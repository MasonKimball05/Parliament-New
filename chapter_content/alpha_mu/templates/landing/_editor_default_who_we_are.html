Beta Theta Pi's Alpha Mu chapter calls Samford University in Birmingham, Alabama home. We are a men's fraternity grounded in the ideals of the Men of Principle — a commitment to intellectual growth, genuine friendship, and moral character.

Our chapter brings together students from across Samford with a shared belief that fraternity, at its best, makes men better — as students, as leaders, and as members of their communities.
