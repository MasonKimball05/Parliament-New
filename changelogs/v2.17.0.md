# v2.17.0 — Slating UX Rework

**Status:** In Development
**Date:** 2026-05-25

---

## Overview

Major UX overhaul of the officer slating system. The primary goal was to reduce the cognitive overhead of running an election — too many disconnected pages, hidden navigation, and unclear progression made the process feel more complicated than it is.

---

## What Changed

### Progress Stepper
- All slating pages now display a 5-step progress stepper at the top (Setup → Nominations → Deliberation → Voting → Complete)
- Completed steps show a green checkmark; the current step is highlighted in primary color; upcoming steps are grayed out
- Stepper renders on any page where a `period` is in context — automatically shows progress at a glance

### Period Setup — Command Center Redesign
- Page redesigned from a flat settings form with a sidebar link dump into a true command center
- **Status command banner** at top: explains what stage the election is in, what to do next, and surfaces a single context-aware "Advance to Next Stage" button (no more hunting for the status dropdown)
  - Setup → "Open Nominations" (green)
  - Nominations Open → "Close Nominations" (yellow)
  - Nominations Closed → "Start Deliberation" (primary)
  - Deliberation → "Open Voting" (blue, validates slate approval server-side)
  - Voting Open → "Close Voting" (red, posts to close_voting endpoint)
  - Voting Closed → "Publish Results" (purple)
- **Sub-action strip** below the banner shows contextual quick links (e.g., "Build Slate →" during deliberation, "Manage Positions" during setup) so key next actions are always one click away
- Cards upgraded to rounded-xl style with subtle section headers, matching the rest of the site
- Overview stats (Positions, Form Fields, Applications) are now clickable links to their respective pages
- Quick Actions sidebar replaces the old plain button list — actions are highlighted with "Now" badges based on the current stage

### Vote Type Selection (New Field)
- Added `vote_type` field to `SlatingPeriod` (choices: `slate` / `individual`)
- Replaces the confusing "Max Slate Voting Attempts" concept in the UI: chair picks the vote format upfront instead of discovering fallback behavior during a failed vote
- Rendered as two interactive selection cards with Alpine.js binding for immediate visual feedback
- Migration 0163 adds the field (default: `slate`)

### Dashboard Modernization
- Active election period cards updated with more visible status badges and CTA buttons ("Apply Now", "Cast Vote")
- All cards updated to `rounded-xl` border style consistent with the rest of the site
- Section headers changed to uppercase tracking-wide style matching other Parliament pages
