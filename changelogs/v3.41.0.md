## 🟢 House map: "N unassigned" opens a quick-manage panel

**Asked (Mason, 10-01-26):** make "x unassigned" on the house map clickable
for whoever can manage houses, opening a list of who isn't in a house where
the house and bigs/littles can be set quickly. Don't count the original
founders. Nobody knows what houses they were in.

### What changed
- **Count.** Leaves out the 1800s original founders (v3.40.0) and Removed
  members. It's now computed from the member rows the page already loads,
  so it costs one query fewer than the old `COUNT`.
- **Panel.** For house managers (officers, admins, historian chairs, the
  same check as `set_member_house`) the badge is a button. Each unassigned
  member gets a house picker, a big picker, and their littles (remove with
  ×, add from a picker, with a confirm if that little already has a big).
  Changes save as you make them; closing the panel reloads the trees. Other
  members still see a plain badge and get no panel data in the page.
- **New `set_member_big` endpoint** (`/officers/members/<id>/set-big/`):
  one write covers bigs and littles. Same permission as setting a house,
  refuses loops in the family tree, inherits the big's house like the
  profile does, and logs to the activity log.
- **Pledge bigs stay with education.** A pledge with a pairing on the
  education dashboard (draft or revealed) is read-only here and refused by
  the endpoint, so a draft can't leak or be overwritten.
