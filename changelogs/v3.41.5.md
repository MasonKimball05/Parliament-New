## 🟢 Amendment editor: "Remove entire section" button

**Asked (Mason, 10-02-26):** the chapter is removing the VPA and VPRM, so some
sections that only concern them are being struck completely. "Add a button to
remove a section from the constitution/bylaws if that is what the author
wants."

The editor could already do this: clearing the text box (with no clause
filled in) records a whole-section deletion. It just wasn't discoverable.

**Change** (the amendment editor on both the edit page and the resolution
page): a red **Remove entire section** button next to "Reset to original".
It clears the text and the clause field; the tracked-changes panel shows the
whole section struck through and the "Deleting entire section" notice appears.
Then **Save Amendment** as usual. "Reset to original" undoes it.

**What happens when the resolution passes** (unchanged): the section's text is
cleared and the section is switched off, with "Deleted by resolution: …" as
the reason. The old text is kept in the section's revision history. Nothing
changes in the live document until the resolution is marked passed.

## 🟡 Resolution page: the deletion notice never showed

`resolution_detail.html`'s editor called `getSelectedType()`, which no longer
exists, so it threw on every keystroke and the "Deleting entire section"
notice never appeared there. It now uses the same test as the edit page.

## 🟡 Edit page: two queries per amendment

Reported by Mason from the performance panel: `amendment.section.full_identifier`
fired an Article and a GoverningDocument query per amendment (7 amendments:
14 queries). `edit_resolution` now prefetches
`amendments__section__article__document`. Measured: 31 → 19 queries with 8
amendments, and the count no longer grows with the number of amendments.

**Tests:** `EditPageQueryCountTests` in `test_cnb_amendment_preview.py`. The
button was checked in a real browser on both pages.
