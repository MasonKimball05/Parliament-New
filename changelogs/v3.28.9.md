  name, for the same concept).

## Two design decisions Mason made this round (09-02-26)

**Visibility: Kai committee only.** A commendation is reviewed by the
committee the same way discipline reports and the old accommodation
requests were — nothing is shown to the chapter automatically, and
nothing is shown to the commended member automatically. There is
deliberately **no honoree-facing view** in this release; if the
committee wants to relay a commendation to the person it's about, that
happens outside the app today. Building that view is a natural follow-up
but is new scope beyond what was asked for.

**Submitter anonymity: the submitter's choice, and NOT a committee-side
redaction mechanism.** `is_submitter_anonymous` is informational — shown
on the committee's detail page as an instruction for whenever they do
relay a commendation, in-app or out. Unlike `KaiReport`'s
`can_view_submitter_identity` gate, nothing here hides the submitter
*from the committee*: a reviewer with `can_view_report_details` always
sees who submitted a commendation. `test_committee_always_sees_the_submitter_with_full_details_permission`
pins this explicitly, since it's the one place this feature could be
mistaken for behaving like `KaiReport`'s redaction and doesn't.

## Everything else carried over unchanged from v3.28.8's design

The toggle between the discipline and commendation forms (on both
`submit_report.html`/`submit_commendation.html` and the form builder),
the shared `KaiFormField` custom-field system scoped by `form_type`,
reuse of the Kai committee's `KaiMemberPermission` grants rather than a
second access-control surface, the private file-serving views
(`kai_commendations/` in `PRIVATE_MEDIA_PREFIXES`, gated by
`_user_may_read_kai_commendation` — submitter or a permitted reviewer,
**not** the commended member, matching the visibility decision above),
and the deliberate non-registration in `/admin/`. Full reasoning for all
of it is in `src/models/kai_commendations.py`'s module docstring (which
also explains, again, why this is a separate model from `KaiReport`
rather than a flag on it — a commendation has no accused and none of
`KaiReport`'s adversarial-parties machinery applies).
