## The bug

`committee_dashboard_links()` (`v3.29.0`) built the Recruitment and
Education quick links by mirroring each dashboard's own access
predicate exactly — and both of those predicates allow a chapter-wide
bypass for management purposes: `recruitment_dashboard`'s `_user_access()`
OR's in `user.is_admin`, and `education_home`'s
`_education_committee_or_404()` OR's in `getattr(user, 'is_officer',
False)`. Mirroring the gate exactly meant mirroring the bypass, so any
admin (Recruitment) or officer (Education) saw a home-page shortcut to
a committee they have no actual tie to.

That bypass is correct on the dashboards themselves — an admin or
officer navigating there directly to help out or troubleshoot is a real
use case. It's wrong on the *shortcut*: a quick link says "here's your
committee," and showing it to someone who's neither chair, member,
advisor, nor role-holder of that specific committee is misleading, even
though the page it points to would happily render for them.

## Fix

`src/view/home.py` — Recruitment and Education now check only
committee-specific signals:

- **Recruitment:** chair, member, advisor, or holder of the committee's
  linked Role. `user.is_admin` removed.
- **Education:** chair only — it has no formal "member" concept beyond
  chairs (`_education_committee_or_404` never checked one).
  `getattr(user, 'is_officer', False)` removed.

**Service Hours is unaffected.** It was never a bug — Service Hours has
no committee at all (per the existing comment, "no service committee
model concept"), it's gated purely by the VPP Role, and admin access
there is the dashboard's actual intended rule (`@vpp_required`), not a
bypass of a narrower one. Kai was already correct — it's gated by
`KaiMemberPermission` grants, not a role bypass, which is exactly why
Mason didn't see it despite having admin.
