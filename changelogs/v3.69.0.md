## 🟢 Slating: rounds as the bylaws run them — run-offs, office-by-office, floor nominations (Mason, 10-29-26)

Bylaws Art. II §3, plus the pending resolution "On the Adjustment of the
Slating Process to Better Enable Chapter Feedback" (run-off positions; the tie
rule is the final wording: the chapter decides). One engine,
`src/slating_rounds.py`, used by the web's slating pages and the app.

- **The first ballot is always the full slate** (pass/fail, the period's %).
  - Passing it elects every slated nominee except run-offs and
    floor-nominations positions, and affirms both run-off candidates.
  - It used to elect no one. Closing a slate vote set `slate.passed` and
    nothing else, so the results page and the officer transition read winners
    off the slate as a whole, and a run-off candidate overwrote the primary.
- **After that the chair picks each round:** the full slate again (for what's
  undecided), or **positions**, ticked one at a time in slate order as the
  bylaws run it, or several at once.
  - The round's threshold defaults to the period's, with 51% for a final
    ballot. Whether members can abstain is set per round.
  - The setup stays on the round (`SlatingRound.ballot`), and members see only
    those positions.
- **A position on a ballot is one of:**
  - **pass/fail** on its nominee. A failure goes back to the committee to
    renominate.
  - a **run-off**: pick one of the two.
  - **floor nominations**: pick one of the accepted nominees, which the chair
    can narrow to the top two.
  - A pick-one elects the leader only with at least the threshold of the votes
    cast for candidates and no tie.
- **Floor nominations:** `SlatingPosition.open_nominations`, switched on per
  position. `SlatingNominee` rows are added by the chair and must be marked
  accepted to go on a ballot.
- **The chapter's own decision:** the chair records a winner for a position (a
  tie, a tabled run-off) or leaves it unfilled (`left_unfilled`). A reason is
  required and goes in the activity log.
- **Web, setup page:**
  - a "Next round" form (full slate or positions; threshold, abstain, nominees)
  - a Positions panel showing each position's state, plus floor nominations
    and the chapter's decision.
- **Web, elsewhere:**
  - The slate ballot lists one row per position, a run-off with both names.
  - The per-position ballot shows pick-one positions.
  - Paper ballots count run-off candidates and nominees.
  - Results start with who holds each office.
  - The officer transition reads the winners, so write-ins work too.
- `src/view/slating/period_setup.open_voting_round` is the web's Open voting,
  shared with the app.
- **App:** the slating chair's "Run the vote" screen (`slating/periods/<id>/chair/`:
  open, close, positions), and the member ballot shows run-offs and floor
  nominations.
- Tests: `src/tests/slating/test_rounds.py` (the bylaws flow end to end), and
  mobile `ChairAppTests`. Earlier slating tests updated for position-keyed
  ballots (`vote_<position id>`, `approve_c<id>` paper fields).
