## 🟢 Resolution page: seven additions and a live indicator

**Asked (Mason, 10-02-26):** from a list of ideas for the resolution pages,
"1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, and 9 look good", then "can you also add an indicator that
the websocket is working/live in the top of the screen too?"

### 1. Removing a section closes the numbering gap
"Remove entire section" (v3.41.5) used to leave the section in place, emptied
and switched off, so an article read § 1, § 2, § 4. Now, when the resolution
passes, the removed section is taken out of the document and the later
sections of that article move up one.

- The amendment says so before anyone votes: "Current §§ 3–4 move up to 2–3."
- A passed amendment keeps the citation it was written against
  (`identifier_snapshot`), so the record still reads "Art. III § 3" after § 4
  has become § 3.
- The removed section is kept in the database (`removed_at`,
  `former_number`) for history and the as-of view. `Section.objects` no longer
  returns it; `Section.all_objects` does.
- If the later numbers can't be shifted (non-numeric numbering such as
  "2a"), nothing is renumbered and the section stays as a switched-off entry,
  as before.
- `apply_amendments` now records every citation first, applies text changes,
  and does removals last, so two removals in one article, or a removal plus an
  edit to a later section, land on the right sections.
- Only sections removed from v3.44.0 on are affected. Sections switched off
  earlier keep their numbers.

### 2. The amendment popup is locked while someone is in it
Opening an existing amendment to edit takes a lock on it, the same way the
title and body fields lock. Anyone else sees "<name> is editing this
amendment" on its row and can't open it until they finish. Locks expire on
their own after 75 seconds without a heartbeat.

### 3. Chair "take over" for a stuck lock
The C&B chair gets a **Take over** button on any field or amendment someone
else holds. The other person gets a notice saying who took over; what they
typed is still on their screen so it can be copied, but it will not save.
Chair only, enforced on the server.

### 4. Edit a proposed article, section or rename in place
Each unapplied row in "New Articles, Sections & Renames" has an **Edit**
button that reopens the maker filled in. Before this the only way to fix a
typo was to remove the proposal and add it again.

### 6. "How the document would read" preview
A **Preview Document** button on the edit page and the resolution page shows
the whole document as it would stand if the resolution passed: new articles
and sections marked, removed sections struck, renumbered ones labelled "was
Article III" / "was § 3", changed text highlighted. A toggle hides the marks
for a clean copy.

It is not an estimate: `src/cnb_projection.py` runs the real apply code
inside a transaction and rolls it back, so the preview and the pass can't
disagree. Nothing is saved. If the resolution can't be applied as it stands,
the page says why.

### 8. Who is editing, on the resolution's own page
The resolution page now shows "<name> is editing this resolution" while
anyone has the edit page open, and updates the amendments and structural
changes in place as they are saved. Viewers connect to a read-only socket
(`/ws/cnb/resolutions/<id>/watch/`): they receive presence and "something
changed" notices only, never draft text, and can't send anything.

### 9. Rename an article directly
Manage Document has a rename panel for articles, for fixing a typo without a
resolution. Chair only, written to the activity log with the old and new
title. Renames that change meaning should still go through a resolution.

### Live indicator
A pill at the top of the edit page and the resolution page: **Live** (green
dot) when the socket is connected, **Connecting…** on load, and
**Reconnecting…** when it has dropped (it retries by itself). While it isn't
Live, saving still works; you just won't see other people's changes until it
reconnects.

## Known limits
- Cross-references in other sections' text are still listed, not rewritten,
  when a removal renumbers (same as v3.43.0).
- The preview covers the documents the viewer is allowed to see.
- Production websockets need the proxy to pass the Upgrade headers. If the
  pill never turns green on the server, that is where to look.
