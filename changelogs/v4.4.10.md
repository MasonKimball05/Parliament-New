## Slating: interview notes survive a dropped connection

Mason's connection dropped while he was taking interview notes. He didn't
notice until he pressed Save, and pages of notes were lost. Now, on the
record-interview page (website) and the interview notes screen (app):

- **A copy is kept on your device as you type.** If the page is closed,
  reloaded or the save fails, the next time you open that interview the
  unsaved notes are put back, with a note saying when they were kept and a
  **Use the saved version instead** option. The copy is deleted as soon as
  the server has the same text, so confidential notes don't stay on a shared
  computer or phone. Anything older than 14 days is dropped, and the app
  deletes its copies when you sign out.
- **A draft is saved to the server every 30 seconds** while you're online
  and something has changed ("Draft saved to the server at 2:14 PM" under
  the button). A draft doesn't mark the interview completed, change the
  application's status or appear in the activity log; Save Interview Notes
  still does all of that.
- **Save checks the connection first (website).** If the server can't be
  reached, nothing is submitted and the page says so. The notes stay on the
  page and on the device, ready to save when you're back online. Before, the
  browser left the page for an error screen and the notes went with it. In
  the app, a failed save already kept the notes on screen; now they're also
  kept on the phone.
- **No overwriting someone else's notes.** If another committee member saved
  the same interview after you opened it, the background save stops instead
  of replacing their notes, and says so. Your notes stay on your device.

Without JavaScript the website form posts exactly as before.
