## App sign-in: a full chapter on the house Wi-Fi isn't turned away

Everyone at chapter shares one public IP. The app's sign-in endpoints allowed
10 requests a minute **per IP** (as well as 10 per device), so when the
chapter signed in together the 11th phone in that minute was refused with
"too many requests". A load test of 36 members signing in at once (24 on the
website, 12 in the app) lost 4 app sign-ins this way.

Per IP, the limits are now 60 a minute for sign-in, the 2-step code and
passkeys, and 120 for token refresh. **Per device, nothing changed** (10 a
minute for sign-in and the code, 20 for passkeys, 30 for refresh), so one
phone or script hammering sign-in is still stopped at the same point as
before. Rates can still be overridden in `MOBILE_AUTH_THROTTLE_RATES`,
per scope (`login`) or per kind (`login_ip`, `login_device`).

## App: vote with your passkey

On the website, every ballot already offered a passkey ("Use a passkey
instead" / "Confirm with a passkey instead") next to the password for anyone with a passkey (since v3.13.3), and the server accepted
one from the app too. The app's ballots never showed the button, so members
had to type their password.

Chapter and committee ballots and the slating ballot (the slate,
position-by-position, run-offs and floor nominations) now have **Vote with
passkey** under the cast button, when you have a passkey and the phone can use it.
On the slating ballot, "Cast your ballot?" is asked first, then Face ID or
Touch ID. The confirmation works once, for 2 minutes, on that phone only. If
you close the passkey sheet nothing is sent, and the password still works.

Failed passkey confirmations have their own limit (5 in 15 minutes, shared
with the website), separate from the 10 wrong vote passwords.

## Load test before chapter (nothing else changed)

Run on a local copy against daphne, with fresh accounts and made-up
passwords. A slate vote went through four rounds: the full slate; then
President, VP (run-off) and an open-nominations seat; then Secretary, House
Manager (abstain allowed) and the open seat narrowed from four nominees to
three; then the top two at 51%. Results were published and opened by
everyone at once.

- **Clean run, 36 members:** after this fix, everyone signed in and voted in
  every round. The vote counts in the database matched the voters exactly,
  with no server errors and no one locked out or blocked.
- **Messy run, 36 members:** each member mistyped their password 0–3 times
  first (48 typos), entered the wrong vote password 0–2 times a round, and
  reloaded the ballot over and over. A third double-tapped Submit (two
  submits at once), and the app's hub was polled throughout. **No server
  errors, no duplicate ballots** (a double tap counts once), and every round
  had exactly one ballot per member who voted.
- **But** without the chapter's IP on the allowlist, the login lockout of 5
  failed sign-ins per IP in 15 minutes locked the whole house out after the
  first few typos: 11 of 36 couldn't sign in for 15 minutes. With
  `127.0.0.1` allowlisted (standing in for the house IP), all 36 got in.
  **Add the chapter's public IP under Admin → IP Whitelist Entries** before a
  vote. That needs no deploy. It must be the exact address (a range like
  `/24` isn't matched, despite the field's help text). The allowlist doesn't
  lift the app's per-IP limit above, which is why that one was raised.
