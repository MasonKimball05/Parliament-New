## 🟠 `add_amendment` had no login check (new in v3.45.0)

### What was wrong
v3.45.0 added the helper `_propose_section_title` to `src/view/officer/cnb.py`
directly above `add_amendment`, between that view and its first decorator. So
`@login_required` ended up on the helper and the view had none.

### What it did
A signed-out POST to `/cnb/resolutions/<id>/amendment/add/` reached the view
body and crashed on `AnonymousUser.has_cnb_permission`: a 500 instead of the
login page. **Nothing was saved.** That is only because the first line that
touches the user happens to raise; the view itself had no gate. A signed-in
member who is not an editor was refused as before.

### Fix
The decorator is back on `add_amendment` and off the helper.

## 🟡 Two older views also crashed for a signed-out visitor

Found by the new sweep (below). Neither saved or showed anything.

- **`/committee/<code>/manage-members/`**: `@committee_chair_required` was
  stacked above `@login_required`, and it read `request.user.is_admin` first.
  500 for a signed-out request when the committee exists.
  `committee_chair_required` and `kai_chair_required` now send a signed-out
  visitor to the login page themselves, like the other decorators in
  `src/decorators.py`.
- **`/csp-report/`** (public and CSRF-exempt on purpose): a 500 for a
  form-encoded body, for JSON that is not an object (`[]`, `"a"`, `5`), and for
  a field that is not text (`{"blocked-uri": 5}`). The first two are now a 400.
  The third is stored as text. An ordinary report is handled as before.

## 🟡 Landing-page photo captions were printed as HTML

`_replace_photo_tags` in `src/view/landing.py` turns `[photo:ID]` into a
`<figure>` inside the three rich-text sections, which are printed with `|safe`.
The section HTML is cleaned with bleach when saved. The caption is not, and it
went into the `<figcaption>` and the `alt="..."` as typed.

- Anyone who can open the landing-page editor (officers, chairs, advisors)
  could add markup to the public home page that the editor's own cleaning
  would have removed. The CSP still blocks scripts and forms that post to
  another site.
- An ordinary caption with a quote mark, `<` or `&` broke its own markup.

The caption and the image URL are now escaped.

## 🟡 Very long amendment texts are no longer compared word by word

`src/cnb_amendment_text.py` (v3.45.0) compares the old and new section text on
every load of the print preview. The cost grows with the square of the length,
and nothing limits how long a proposed text may be. Measured on the 2-core test
machine: 20,000 words of ordinary prose took 1.5 s; 60,000 words of repetitive
text took over three minutes.

Above `MAX_DIFF_WORDS = 6000` (old plus new) the amendment is now stated as one
change of the whole text, the same as a rewrite. The longest seeded section is
1,373 words, so real sections are not affected.

## New guard: every route, signed out

`src/tests/guards/test_anonymous_access.py` requests every route signed out
(GET and POST, 1,324 routes) against an empty database.

- Each must redirect to the login page or answer 401 / 403 / 405, or be listed
  in `PUBLIC` in that file with the reason (31 routes: login, password reset,
  the public pages, token links, email pixels, the CSP receiver, the health
  check).
- None may return a 5xx.
- A `PUBLIC` entry that is gone or has become gated fails the test.

It does not check that the wrong signed-in member is refused. The DRF router,
the Django admin's regex routes and the honeypot routes are skipped.
