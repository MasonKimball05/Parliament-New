## Songbook: what "Play all" needs

The app's songbook can now keep a song playing after you leave its page, play
every recording one after another ("Play all"), and play in the background and
from the lock screen (parliament-mobile; it needs a new app build, since
background audio is a native setting). To queue the recordings from the list,
the list API (`GET songbook/`) now gives each song its `audio_ext` and
`updated_at`, as the song's own endpoint already did. Older app builds ignore
them; the new app falls back to `.mp3` without them.

**Test:** `test_songbook_quotes.test_list_detail_and_audio` checks both fields.

### Fixed on the website along the way

- **Preferences wouldn't save while dark mode is off chapter-wide.** `theme`
  is a required field and the form only rendered it with dark mode on, so
  every save failed. The form now posts `theme=light` then.
- **A taken username was a 500** on the Profile page (an IntegrityError at
  save). `save_account_fields` refuses it: "That username is taken."
- **Profile picture checks never ran.** `validate_profile_picture` (JPEG,
  PNG or WebP, at most 5 MB) is a model field validator, and `save()` doesn't
  run those, so the page stored any file. `save_profile_picture` now runs it;
  the page already showed its message.
- **Feedback** now checks page, priority and request type against their
  choices and trims fields to their column lengths, as bug reports have since
  v3.51.6 (a too-long value was a 500 on PostgreSQL).

## Pre-push tests pass on SQLite again

The pre-push hook runs the suite on SQLite, which has no JSON `contains`
lookup, so four app sign-in tests errored there (Postgres and CI were fine).
Spotting a reused, unknown refresh token (`src/mobile/tokens.py`) now scans
the device rows in Python when the database can't do the lookup itself.

## Making an event no longer signs you out of the app

Saving an event with a problem (a missing title, a bad time) signed the app
out. The server's form errors used the code `invalid`, and the app reads a bare
`invalid` as "this phone's sign-in is dead" (it's what a failed token refresh
says). Fixed on both sides:

- **Server:** form errors from the app's APIs are now `invalid_input` (agendas,
  minutes, committee minutes, officer events and tools, pledge tasks,
  preferences, songbook, slating), so the app already on phones stops signing
  out as soon as this deploys. `tests/mobile/test_error_codes.py` fails if a
  view outside auth uses any of the app's sign-out codes again.
- **App:** those codes only mean signed out on a 401 or 403, so a form's 400
  can't sign anyone out even if a code slips through.

## Ready for the App Store

What App Review checks, and what the chapter decided along the way.
`parliament-mobile/docs/APP_STORE_SUBMISSION.md` has every App Store Connect
answer.

### Privacy policy, terms and support (public pages)

`/privacy/`, `/terms/` and `/support/` (src/view/legal.py, templates/legal/)
load signed out and are linked from the landing page footer, the sign-in page
and the app (sign-in screen and Settings → About). The privacy policy was
written from an inventory of what the code actually stores, shares and keeps;
change it (and `EFFECTIVE_DATE`) whenever that changes. The terms carry the
zero-tolerance policy Apple asks of apps with member-posted content, and say
member posts are the poster's own, not the chapter's. The support page's form
lands in the officers' Contact messages. All three are always allowed for
pledges, and `test_anonymous_access` lists them as public.

### Deleting your account (Apple guideline 5.1.1(v))

From the app (Settings → Delete account) or the website (Preferences), with
the password or a passkey. The chapter keeps its membership record; the
account and personal data go:

- **At once:** signed out everywhere and can't sign in (password unusable,
  app devices revoked, passkeys, 2FA, API and calendar tokens, web push and
  sessions deleted). Confirmation email with the purge date; admins notified.
- **After 30 days** (nightly Celery task): email, phone, socials, photo, bio,
  studies, sign-in history, page visits, preferences and app devices erased;
  IPs nulled on their activity rows; username becomes `deleted-<id>`.
- **Kept:** name, roll number, pledge class, family line, house, initiation
  and graduation, roles and role history, and every vote, attendance,
  minute, Kai case and message. They show as a "Former member" (like the
  founders): in the directory's alumni view and the house map, never in
  active rosters, quorum, ballots or notification audiences (the user
  queryset drops them from any `member_status` filter;
  `ParliamentUser.objects.including_closed()` opts out).
- **Admins** (Admin v2 → People → Account deletions): Restore within 30 days,
  and after the purge Rebuild an account onto the kept record, both only at
  the member's request and written to the audit log. Admins can't delete
  their own account.

The passkey confirmation has its own one-shot grant (`delete_grant`), so a
vote's passkey grant can't confirm a deletion, or the reverse.

### Reporting and muting (Apple guideline 1.2)

- **Chat:** long-press another member's message to report it or mute them.
  Reports go to officers who can read that channel, or, where no officer can
  (Kai's committee channel), to its committee's chairs; nobody else sees the
  text or the reporter. Three reporters hide a message until reviewed.
  Muting hides a member's messages, unreads and notifications for you only;
  they aren't told. Officers review in Officer tools → Chat reports (and
  `/chats/reports/` on the web).
- **Quote book:** any member can report a quote (it stays up; officers are
  notified; three reporters hide it). The quoted member's own flag works as
  before.
- docs/CONFIDENTIALITY_MATRIX.md has a new "Chat and Quote Book" section.

### App Review account

`APP_REVIEW_USERNAMES=appreview`, then `manage.py create_app_review_account`
(prints the password once; `--disable` after review). A plain member that
skips 2FA, never sees other members' email or phone (directory, cards,
export, search), is left out of the directory, rosters, counts and the house
map, and can't cast ballots. No migration (src/app_review.py).

### Sign-in locations stay on our server

src/geo_utils.py now reads MaxMind's GeoLite2 City database locally
(`maxminddb`, `GEOIP_CITY_DB`) instead of sending sign-in IPs to ip-api.com
over plain HTTP. Same results, minus the ISP name; no database means no
location and no network fallback. Setup and weekly updates: DEPLOYMENT.md
§5a. The policy carries MaxMind's attribution.

### The app

New screens: Delete account, Muted members, Chat reports (officers), plus
report actions in chat and the quote book, and Privacy / Terms / Support /
Forgot password links. The iOS privacy manifest now declares the 12 kinds of
data the app collects (it listed none). That manifest is native, so it ships
with the next build.
