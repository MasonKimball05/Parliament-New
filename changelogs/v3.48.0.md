## 🟢 Quizzes: full points for doing it, or points by % right

Mason, 10-16-26: "One where the pledges can get full points just for doing the
quiz and another where they get a certain number of points proportional to the
% they get right. So if a quiz is 10 points and they get 7 right it's 7 points."

### What changed
- **New quiz setting "Quiz points"** on the Add Task modal and the Edit Task
  page (shown only when Type is Quiz):
  - *Full points for completing it*: the default, and how every quiz worked before.
  - *Points by % right*: points × fraction right, rounded half up to a whole
    point (7.5 → 8), never more than the quiz's points.
- **Where "% right" comes from** (Mason: "a bit of both"):
  1. the score a chair typed, if the quiz is scored out of a total and this
     pledge has a score (score ÷ max score). That's the override, e.g. for partial credit;
  2. otherwise the answers marked right ÷ number of questions. A question not
     marked yet counts as not right;
  3. if there's neither, full points.
- **Still needs Completed** (Mason's call): a quiz marked Pending, Incomplete
  or Waived earns 0 in both modes, as before.
- **Same number everywhere.** The dashboard's points, the per-pledge page and
  the pledge's own My Tasks (and "View as pledge") all use one helper,
  `src/pledge_points.py`. My Tasks and the pledge page show "7 of 10 pts" on a
  by-% quiz once it's completed; before that, My Tasks says "Up to 10 pts, by % right".
- Duplicating a task copies the setting. Any non-quiz type is always saved as
  `full`.
- Cost: two small grouped queries per page, only when at least one by-% quiz exists.
