## 🟢 Location check-in (Mason, 10-27-26)

An officer gives an attendance event a check-in area (a point and a radius).
A member whose phone is inside it while check-in is open is marked present, as
if they had scanned the QR code. That counts for voting eligibility too (the
chapter's decision). The app watches the areas as iOS geofences, so with
"Always" location access it works with Parliament closed; with "While using"
it checks in when the app is opened at the event.

### Rules (`src/location_checkin.py`)
- Window: an hour before the start until three hours after (events have no end
  time).
- Distance: inside the radius, allowing up to 50 m of the fix's own GPS
  uncertainty; fixes worse than ±150 m are refused.
- Active members only; not once attendance is finalized; never overwrites
  present/late (a QR scan or an officer's marking stays as it was).
- The attendance note says "Checked in by location (N m …, GPS ±M m)", and the
  activity log records it, so officers can see how and override it.
- Behind a new flag, `location_checkin`, plus `attendance_tracking` and
  `event_attendance`. **Off by default** (`DISABLED_BY_DEFAULT`), like QR
  check-in. Turn it on in feature flags to use it.

### The check-in key
The app's background task runs while the phone may be locked, when iOS won't
release the sign-in tokens (they're stored WHEN_UNLOCKED). So each phone gets a
second, narrow secret: `POST devices/checkin-key/` (Bearer) returns it once
and stores only its hash (`MobileDevice.checkin_key_hash`). It authenticates
exactly one endpoint, `POST events/<id>/checkin/location/`
(`CheckinKeyAuthentication` / `CheckinKeyAPIView`), and passes the same
`still_allowed` checks as a token (revoked phone, password change, removed
member). The site middlewares resolve its member too (`src/mobile/http.py`), so
lockdown, maintenance and quarantine still apply. The mobile auth guard test
now has a third, explicit kind for it.

### Endpoints (`src/mobile/views_location.py`)
- `GET events/checkin-areas/`: upcoming areas (next 7 days, at most 15), each
  with its window and whether the member is already in.
- `POST events/<id>/checkin/location/` with `{latitude, longitude, accuracy}`.
  Errors are prefixed (`checkin_not_open`, `checkin_too_far`,
  `checkin_inaccurate`, ...), since the app reads some bare codes as signed out.
- Officers and chairs: `PUT/DELETE events/<id>/checkin-area/` (a point and
  radius, or a saved place, optionally `save_as` a new place),
  `GET/POST checkin-places/`, `DELETE checkin-places/<id>/`.
- `GET events/<id>/` adds `checkin_area` and `can_manage_checkin`; `me/` adds
  `features.location_checkin`.

### Website
- The event form's attendance panel has a "Location check-in" section: a map
  (Leaflet 1.9.4, vendored in `static/vendor/leaflet/` with its hashes in
  `.integrity.json`; OpenStreetMap tiles), "Use my location", saved places and
  "Save this spot as". `templates/officer/_checkin_area.html`,
  `static/js/officer/checkin_area_map.js`.
- With the flag off, the form leaves the fields out, so saving never clears an
  area.
- The area is copied to every occurrence of a recurring event and is in
  `SERIES_FIELDS`, so a series edit carries it.
- Saved places (`CheckinPlace`) are copied onto an event, not linked, so
  editing or deleting a place never moves an existing event.
