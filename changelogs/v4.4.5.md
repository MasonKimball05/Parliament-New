## Slating: candidates can sit out their own race

A new election setting, **Candidates can't vote on their own race** (Voting
Configuration on the election's setup page, and in the app's election setup).
Off by default; locked while a vote is open.

- **Whole-slate vote:** anyone on the slate (a nominee, a write-in, a run-off
  candidate) doesn't vote on it. They see "You're on this slate, so you don't
  vote on it" instead of the ballot, on the website and in the app.
- **Position vote** (run-offs, floor nominations): a candidate's own position
  is left off their ballot, with a note saying why; they vote on the rest.
- **Nothing is recorded for them**: no ballot and no abstention. So it works
  the same whether or not abstaining is allowed, turnout counts only people
  who could vote, and pass/fail (approve ÷ approve + reject) is decided by
  everyone else's votes.

Enforced where every vote is written (`src/slating_ballots.py`), so a forged
request can't get around it. No migration: it is stored in the election's
existing settings.

## GroupMe: "Check connection" never posts, and a site-wide switch

On 11-05-26 the "Send test" button posted a test message in two chapter
GroupMes' main chats, where everyone saw it. A test must never post.

- **"Send test" is now "Check connection"** (Officers → GroupMe, and the app's
  GroupMe screen). It asks GroupMe whether the channel's bot still exists and
  which group it is in, using the connected GroupMe account, and **never
  posts anything**. The old route runs the check too, so an older app build's
  "Send test" button can't post either.
- **A new "GroupMe posting" feature flag** (Admin v2 → Feature Flags). Every
  GroupMe post goes through one function, which refuses while the flag is
  off, so nothing can post to any group. Seeded on, so nothing changes until
  it is turned off.
- **Where posts land is spelled out:** the GroupMe page, the announcement
  form and the event form say posts appear in the group's main chat,
  visible to everyone in it, and name the group. A GroupMe bot can't post
  only in an Announcements topic.
- **Waits for the email step.** An announcement created with "send email"
  went to GroupMe before the officer finished the email confirmation page.
  Its GroupMe post now waits until the emails are sent or skipped (or 30
  minutes, if the page is abandoned), and the 5-minute sweep respects that.
- **Says who posted it.** Announcement posts read "Posted by {name}" under
  the title; event reminders name who put the event on the calendar.
- **Deleting on Parliament deletes it on GroupMe.** Deleting an announcement,
  or an event whose reminder went out, deletes that post from the GroupMe
  group too: from the officer pages, the app, Admin v2 and a recurring
  series alike. A post that hadn't gone out yet is cancelled. The delete
  confirmation says so when there's a post.
  - GroupMe only lets a group's **owner or admin** delete the bot's posts, so
    the connected GroupMe account must be one. "Check connection" now says
    whether it is.
  - If GroupMe refuses (not an admin, no account connected, or the post is too
    old to find), Parliament's delete still happens and the GroupMe log says
    why the post wasn't removed. Deleted posts show as "Deleted" in the log.
  - A bot's post comes back with no id, so Parliament finds it in the group's
    recent messages right after posting and keeps the id.
- **Tests can't reach the real GroupMe.** During a test run every GroupMe
  call is refused unless the test replaced it with the fake GroupMe.

## Slating: ready for Saturday

Three full elections were clicked through on a test server (chair, manager,
committee, voters, candidates) ahead of the 11-07-26 election. Everything they
found is fixed below, website and app alike.

**Votes count correctly**

- **Closing voting is final.** A ballot still in flight when the chair closes
  the round is refused ("Voting closed before your ballot was recorded")
  instead of landing after the count. Closing twice, or opening, closing,
  deciding and voiding at the same moment, can't overlap.
- **Results don't change after a round closes.** The results page, the results
  document and the app read the closed round's stored count (with the
  approval % it used), rather than recounting live. This fixes the 0/0 counts
  and both run-off candidates showing "Elected".
- **Required Approval %** must be 51–100 and is fixed once the first round
  opens.
- **No slate changes mid-round.** Swapping a candidate is refused while a
  round including that position is open ("Pause or close voting before
  changing the slate"). In-person results are refused once voting has started
  online.
- **Who may vote is re-checked when the ballot is cast** (Active or Inactive
  only), and a member not marked present gets a clear message.

**The election moves in order**

- **Status steps are fixed:** setup → nominations open → closed →
  deliberation → voting open (pause back to deliberation) → voting closed →
  results published (unpublish back) → archived. No skipping.
- **Results can't be published while voting is open.** One publish path for
  the setup page, the results page and the app, saying what's still
  undecided when it can't.
- **The schedule** (if dates are set) takes one step at a time through the
  same rules, and never closes a round the chair opened late.
  Recommendation for Saturday: leave the dates blank and open and close voting
  by hand.
- **Recording the chapter's decision** for the last open position closes
  voting. It's allowed only in deliberation or after voting closes, and
  overriding an elected officer asks first.
- **A closed round no longer shows "Voting paused / Void this round."**

**Fair committee**

- **No one running can be on the slating committee:** a committee member
  or the slating manager can't apply or be added as a write-in or floor
  nominee, and an applicant can't be added to the committee or made the
  manager (the pickers leave them out). Pledges can apply.
- **Only the committee admin or the slating manager can transfer the
  committee**; a site admin only before the election is locked.
- **Secret ballot in the Django admin:** vote and ballot lists show no
  timestamps, sorting, actions or row links, and a single vote's or ballot's
  page can't be opened (their ids line up, so the two pages side by side
  showed how someone voted).

**Easier on the day**

- **Attendance:** faster marking, a running "X present" count and a link
  next to Open voting. Voting won't open with nobody present.
- **Confirmations** before approving a slate ("1 of 2 filled; VPP unfilled")
  and opening voting ("round N, full slate, X members present").
- **An approved slate is read-only**; copy it to start over.
- **Live turnout:** "N of M present have voted." While voting is open,
  results read "Currently X% approve — not final until you close voting."
- **Results** lead with the officers elected and how each position was
  decided (slate, run-off, chapter, in person), with the round history.
- **Officer transition** shows a review page of every role change before
  anything happens. A transition scheduled for later no longer runs at
  once, and odd member ids no longer cause an error.
- **Create election** keeps the Slating Manager and schedule dates, and a
  script error on that page is fixed.
- **A second full run-through** confirmed the fixes and caught the rest:
  Save Draft on an unfinished application is no longer blocked by the
  browser's required-field checks; Approve Slate now asks first; a ballot
  sent after the close says "Voting closed before your ballot was recorded";
  "Record the chapter's decision" only shows where it can be used (between
  rounds or after voting closes, not for a position on the open ballot); and
  "View Live Results" reads "View Results" once voting has closed.

## Forms with a field named "action" submit properly

Forms with file uploads are resubmitted by the page's script (to refresh the
security token). On a form with a field named `action` (slating apply), that
script posted to a broken address. It also dropped the pressed button, so
"Submit Application" saved a draft. Both are fixed for every form.

## Location check-in: find an address, then drag and size the circle

On the event form's location check-in map (website; officers set the
check-in area there):

- **Search an address or place** above the map and press Find (or Enter). One
  match drops the pin there; several show a short list to pick from. Then
  drag the pin to the exact spot. The search goes through Parliament's server
  to OpenStreetMap's free address search (so the officer's own location and
  IP never reach it), at most once a second for the whole site, with results
  kept for a month.
- **Circle size is a slider** (25–500 m) next to a number box for anything
  from 25 to 1000 m, instead of the fixed list; the map zooms to show the
  whole circle. "Turn off" removes the check-in area.
- Tests never call the address search (it's refused during a test run, like
  GroupMe).

## Committee home: Recent Votes shows the votes

A committee's Recent Votes listed individual members' ballots (no title, so
blank rows, one per member) and the Votes tile counted ballots. It now lists
the committee's votes themselves, newest first, each marked Open, Passed or
Did not pass, and opens the vote (if open) or its result. The Votes tile
counts votes and "active" counts the ones open now, by the same rule as the
Voting page. Old look and refreshed look alike.

## Slating: what the committee's members can do

Members of an election's slating committee (not just its chairs, admin and
the slating manager) can now:

- **See the results before they're published**, including **which positions
  "no" voters objected to** (Rejection Analysis), on the website's results page
  and in the app ("Results so far (committee)"). While voting is open it says
  nothing is final. Members not on the committee still wait for publishing.
- **Schedule interviews** as well as take the notes.
- **Build and order the slate:** assign candidates and run-off candidates,
  write-ins, notes, preview and copy a slate. **Approving the slate** stays
  with the managers ("A slating manager or chair approves the slate").
- Find all of it from the Slating hub: Applications, Interviews, Slate and
  Results links on the election's card (website), and "Build the slate" in
  the app.

They still have **no setup access**: the election's settings, positions and
form, status changes, attendance, opening and closing voting, editing the
approved slate, in-person results and publishing stay with the slating
manager, the committee admin and chairs.

A "no" vote on the whole slate must still name at least one position it
objects to (website, app and server), and now has a test of its own.
