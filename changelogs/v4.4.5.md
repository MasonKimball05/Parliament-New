## The website refresh, continued

With the refresh on (the "Website Refresh (app look)" switch, or `?ui=refresh`
in one browser), five more pages take the app's look. With it off, every page
is exactly as before, and every form, field, link and permission is kept.

- **Vote:** each bill is a card with its state as a pill (Open, Pending), the
  ballot is the app's list of choices with a radio on the right, then
  "Confirm your password" and Submit Vote. Notices (not open yet, voted,
  turnout) are tinted cards and the officer tabs are a segmented control.
  Voting is tested to work the same.
- **Announcements:** each post is a card with its title and Event pill, who
  posted it and when, the event date, the full text, and the poll and
  documents as rows.
- **Profile:** your photo or initials, name and pills centred at the top, like
  the app, with the settings sections as grouped rows.
- **Calendar:** the month card with round arrows, today in an accent circle
  and color-barred event chips (event, service, recruitment); Upcoming Events
  as a list with date blocks; event details in a rounded panel. Month
  navigation, the month picker, Subscribe, Export, sign-up and excuses work as
  before.
- **Directory:** each section is a grouped card with a row per member
  (initial or photo, name, roll number, contact, roles), with chip-style
  filters; the profile card follows the app's member screen and opens as a
  bottom sheet on phones. Search, sort and filters work as before.

Then nine more pages:

- **A committee's own pages:** the home page shows your roles as pills, its
  numbers as tiles, and its rosters, actions and recent documents and votes
  as grouped rows with icon tiles (chair tools, Add and Remove kept). Minutes
  and Documents list each item as a row with status pills and small action
  buttons; committee Voting and a vote's result page get the app's ballot and
  result styling. A committee vote cast under the refresh is tested to count.
- **Chats:** the channel list as the app's rows (round icons, unread badges);
  admins' edit and delete links kept. The conversation gets the app's colors
  and a round Send button.
- **Service Hours:** progress as colored stat tiles and submissions as rows
  with status pills; the submit form restyled.
- **Preferences:** every on/off setting, including the top bar links, is a
  switch, and saves exactly as before (tested).
- **Songbook:** songs as grouped rows with colored icon tiles and categories
  as chips; a song's page has a cleaner header and a larger lyrics block.

## Slating: candidates can sit out their own race

A new election setting, **Candidates can't vote on their own race** (Voting
Configuration on the election's setup page, and in the app's election setup).
Off by default; locked while a vote is open.

- **Whole-slate vote:** anyone on the slate (a nominee, a write-in, a run-off
  candidate) doesn't vote on it. They see "You're on this slate, so you don't
  vote on it" instead of the ballot, on the website and in the app.
- **Position vote** (run-offs, floor nominations): a candidate's own position
  is left off their ballot, with a note saying why; they vote on the rest.
- **Nothing is recorded for them**: no ballot and no abstention. So it works
  the same whether or not abstaining is allowed, turnout counts only people
  who could vote, and pass/fail (approve ÷ approve + reject) is decided by
  everyone else's votes.

Enforced where every vote is written (`src/slating_ballots.py`), so a forged
request can't get around it. No migration: it is stored in the election's
existing settings.

## GroupMe: "Check connection" never posts, and a site-wide switch

On 11-05-26 the "Send test" button posted a test message in two chapter
GroupMes' main chats, where everyone saw it. A test must never post.

- **"Send test" is now "Check connection"** (Officers → GroupMe, and the app's
  GroupMe screen). It asks GroupMe whether the channel's bot still exists and
  which group it is in, using the connected GroupMe account, and **never
  posts anything**. The old route runs the check too, so an older app build's
  "Send test" button can't post either.
- **A new "GroupMe posting" feature flag** (Admin v2 → Feature Flags). Every
  GroupMe post goes through one function, which refuses while the flag is
  off, so nothing can post to any group. Seeded on, so nothing changes until
  it is turned off.
- **Where posts land is spelled out:** the GroupMe page, the announcement
  form and the event form say posts appear in the group's main chat,
  visible to everyone in it, and name the group. A GroupMe bot can't post
  only in an Announcements topic.
