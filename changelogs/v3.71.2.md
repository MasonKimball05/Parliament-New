## 🟢 Mobile app: attendance dashboard and polls for officers (Mason, 10-30-26)

### Attendance dashboard
- `officer/attendance-dashboard/?range=` and `officer/attendance-dashboard/<user_id>/`
  serve the website's attendance dashboard and member attendance pages.
- Both now share `attendance_dashboard_context` and `member_attendance_context`
  with the website.
- A member's history no longer runs two queries per event: one query each for
  their records and their excuses.

### Polls
- `officer/announcements/<id>/poll/`:
  - `GET` reads the poll.
  - `PUT` creates it or saves changes.
  - `DELETE` removes it.
- `officer/announcements/<id>/poll/results/` serves the results, with the same
  anonymity rules as the website.
- The save and results rules moved to `src/poll_editing.py` (`save_poll`,
  `poll_results_data`), which the website's `create_or_edit_poll` and
  `poll_results` now use too. Their behaviour is unchanged.

### Tests
- `src/tests/mobile/test_officer_polls.py` covers the website form and the app
  endpoints.
- `AttendanceDashboardTests` is in `test_officer.py`.
