## 🟡 Taking items out of a list read as a chain of renames

### What it looked like
Removing Vice President of Administration and Vice President of Risk
Management from the officer list in Constitution Art. III § 3 printed as:

> (a) Change "Administration" to read "Brotherhood"
> (b) Change "Brotherhood" to read "Finance"
> (c) Change "Finance" to read "Recruitment"
> (d) Change "Risk Management g. Vice President of Recruitment h. …" to read "Education g."

The final text was right. The motion was not what the chapter is voting on.
The same thing gave "Change 7. to read 6." in Bylaws Art. VI § 2, and the
appendix showed lone struck letters on lines of their own.

### Cause
Both the motion (`src/cnb_amendment_text.py`) and the appendix (the print
page's script) compared the old and new text word by word. When an item is
taken out of a lettered list, every item below it moves up a letter, so the
words line up with the wrong items.

### Fix
Both now compare **line by line first**, matching lines by their words
without the list marker ("a.", "2.", "iv.", "(1)"):

- A line whose words are unchanged but whose letter or number changed is not
  an edit. The motion adds one line: "The items that follow are renumbered to
  match." The appendix strikes the old letter and marks the new one.
- A line that is gone is one strike: `Strike "c. Vice President of Administration"`.
- A new line is one addition, located by the line before it:
  `Add after "n. Awards": "o. Communications Chair"`.
- A line that was edited is compared word by word inside that line, as before.
- A line replaced by a different line is one change of the whole line.

The officer-list example now prints:

> Shall strike from Article III, Section 3 of the Constitution (Chapter Officers) as follows:
> (a) Strike "c. Vice President of Administration"
> (b) Strike "f. Vice President of Risk Management"
> The items that follow are renumbered to match.

Checked in Chromium on the print preview (motion and appendix).
