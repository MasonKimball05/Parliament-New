## What was stale or missing

concrete gaps, rather than guessing:

- **Poll QR embeds** (added this session, `v3.28.7`) had no mention in
  the Announcements guide's Polls & Surveys section — verified the real
  entry point (`announcement_poll_results.html`'s "QR Code" button →
  `manage_poll_qr`) and the exact button labels ("Get embed link" /
  "Revoke this link") before writing the copy.
- **QR self-check-in for attendance** (`qr_attendance_checkin` feature
  flag, from earlier work) had no guide section at all. Verified the
  entry point (`mark_event_attendance.html`'s "QR self-check-in" card),
  the 15-minute window, and the "unavailable once finalized" behavior
  directly against `event_attendance.py` and the template.
- **Kai Commendations** (`v3.28.9`, built and migration-fixed earlier
  this session) — zero mentions anywhere in the Kai guide, which reads
  entirely as if Kai were only for conduct reports. Verified the form
  fields and their exact labels/help text against `KaiCommendationForm`
  before writing the section.
- **Committee document management** (upload, publish/unpublish to
  chapter, version history, replace file) — a real, working feature
  with no guide coverage at all. Verified visibility rules and
  per-role button gating directly against `committee/documents.py` and
  its template before writing the "what you can do, by role" list.
- **Two orphaned TOC cards**: `guide_legislation` and `guide_committees`
  both exist, render fine, and even link to each other via their own
  prev/next nav — but neither had a card on the main `/guide/` index, so
  neither was discoverable by browsing or by the page's search box.

- `templates/guide/index.html` — two new cards (Legislation & Voting,
  Committees) in the Member Guides grid. Their search keywords were
  added in all three places the page's Alpine.js search needs them kept
  in sync: the card's own `x-show`, the Member Guides section wrapper's
  `any(...)`, and the "no results" message's `any(...)` — missing any
  one of the three would have made the card invisible under some
  combination of scrolling vs. searching.

Deliberately **not** added: a note about the email-warmup/confirmation
mechanism (`warmup_announcement_email`) — its only user-visible effect,
the confirmation popup, is already documented under "Scheduling
Announcements"; and a mention of the new committee dashboard quick
links from `v3.29.0` — those are a home-page shortcut to pages the
guides already describe, not a new capability that needs its own
explanation.
