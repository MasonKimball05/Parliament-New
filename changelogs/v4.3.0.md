## Formerly v3.71.0 (`8f65583`)

### 🟢 Timed location check-in and study hours (VP of Education's request, 10-30-26)

Rules in `src/location_time.py`. Built on location check-in (v3.60.0): the
area, distance and accuracy checks, and the check-in key, are the same.

#### Minimum stay
- An attendance event can require time at the event (`Event.checkin_min_minutes`:
  15 minutes to 2 hours) instead of just arriving.
- The member is marked present once their total time inside the area reaches it.
  Visits add up, so stepping out briefly is fine.
- The same rule as a location check-in applies: only a missing or pending
  attendance row is filled, so an officer's marking stays as it was.
- The window is unchanged: an hour before the start until three hours after.

#### Study hours
- A study hours event (`Event.is_study_hours`) counts the time members spend
  inside its area, from the start for as long as it lasts (`duration_minutes`,
  1 to 12 hours).
- It's a length rather than an end time, so a recurring series carries it to
  every occurrence. It takes no attendance.
- Members can be given a weekly study hours requirement (`StudyHoursRequirement`).

#### How time is counted
- The app reports arriving (a position, checked as before) and leaving (`POST
  events/<id>/checkin/location/leave/`, with the same key).
- It uses iOS geofence enter and exit events, which need "Always" location
  access. With "While using", it reports what it sees each time the app is opened.
- A visit whose "left" never arrives (phone off, no signal) stops counting when
  the window ends, and is marked "didn't report leaving" for officers to check.
- A Celery job every two minutes (`tasks.location_time_tick`) credits members who
  reach the minimum while still inside, and closes those visits.

#### Manual changes
- Add or remove minutes, always with a reason (`LocationTimeAdjustment`, and
  logged).
- Who can: officers and chairs, the education committee's chairs, and members
  given the new **Time** permission (`EducationMemberPermission.can_adjust_time`)
  on the education permissions page.

#### Website
- The event form's location check-in section gains "Time needed at the event",
  "Study hours" and "Study hours last".
- `/officers/events/<id>/time/` (Time & adjustments): everyone's time, each visit,
  and the adjustments. It can add or remove time for anyone, including a member
  whose phone didn't count it. It's linked from the attendance page of a
  minimum-stay event.
- `/committee/<code>/education/study-hours/`: the week's study hours per member
  against their requirement (previous and next week), setting requirements, and
  the week's study hours events. It's linked from the Education dashboard.
- A pledge's My Tasks page shows this week's study hours against their requirement.

#### App API
- `events/checkin-areas/` includes study hours events, plus `timed`,
  `min_minutes`, `study_hours`, `inside` and `minutes` for each.
- `events/<id>/` gains `timed_checkin`: the member's minutes, whether they're
  inside, and the Time & adjustments link for those who may adjust.
- The education pledge detail (`committees/<code>/education/pledges/<pk>/`) gains
  `study`.

#### Tests
- `src/tests/mobile/test_location_time.py` (13).

## Formerly v3.71.3 (`e8e33e3`)

### 🟢 Mobile app: chapter minutes for officers (Mason, 10-30-26)

#### Endpoints
The website's minutes editor, under `officer/minutes/`:
- `GET` lists every session and the events one can be linked to.
- `POST` starts a new session.
- `<id>/`:
  - `GET` reads the sections, the roll call (in roll call order) and the motion
    choices.
  - `DELETE` deletes the minutes. As on the website, officers can delete drafts
    only.
- `<id>/sections/` (`PUT`) saves the write-up and end time.
- `<id>/roll-call/` (`PUT`) saves the roll call and syncs the linked event's
  attendance, including approving the excuses of members marked excused.
- `<id>/event/` (`POST`) links or unlinks an event.
- `<id>/publish/` (`POST`) publishes the PDF with a visibility.
- `<id>/pdf/` (`GET`) is the preview PDF for the app's viewer.

The app validates section types, motion choices and roll call statuses. Members
who aren't on the roll are dropped.

#### Shared with the website
- `src/view/officer/chapter_minutes.py` now has `minutes_editor_state`,
  `create_minutes`, `link_minutes_event`, `save_minutes_sections`,
  `save_roll_call`, `publish_minutes` and `delete_minutes`.
- The web views call these helpers, so the rules are identical.
- Fixed a precedence slip in the linked-event edit reason. A reason typed by the
  user is now kept as written.

#### Other fixes
- The officer portal's Mobile App page URL names are now `officer_mobile_app`
  and `officer_mobile_app_place`. The guard tests reserve `mobile_*` for the
  app's API.
- The check-in-key guard lists `mobile_location_leave`.
- The template `.count` guard allows the Mobile App page's two queryset counts.

#### Tests
- `src/tests/mobile/test_minutes.py` covers:
  - create validation
  - a write-up with a motion
  - the roll call syncing attendance and excuses
  - link, publish and delete (officers drafts only, admins any)
  - members refused
  - the PDF

## Added after the renumbering

### 🟢 Mobile app: meeting agendas and a pledge's My Tasks (Mason, 10-30-26)

#### Meeting agendas
`src/mobile/views_agendas.py`, under `agendas/`. Same gate as the website:
feature flag `meeting_agendas`, and writes need officer or chair.
- `GET agendas/` lists upcoming and past agendas.
  - Members see published ones only.
  - Officers also see drafts and the events an agenda can be linked to.
  - `POST` creates one, with the standard order of business if asked.
- `GET agendas/<id>/` is one agenda and its items. A draft is a 404 for
  members. Officers also get the item types and the presenter choices.
- Officers can also:
  - add an item (`items/`)
  - edit an item (`PATCH`, which keeps any fields it leaves out) or delete it
    (`items/<id>/`)
  - move an item up or down (`items/<id>/move/`)
  - publish the agenda or move it back to draft (`status/`)
  - start the minutes from it (`start-minutes/`). That happens once, and the
    app opens the result at `officer/minutes/<id>/`.
- `me/` `features` gains `meeting_agendas`.
- Moved out of the web views in `src/view/officer/meeting_agenda.py`, which now
  call them too: `visible_agendas`, `create_agenda_record`, `apply_item_fields`,
  `add_item`, `move_item`, `change_status`, `start_minutes`.

#### A pledge's My Tasks
`src/mobile/views_pledge.py`, under `pledge/`. Pledges only, as on the website.
- `GET pledge/tasks/` is `build_pledge_tasks_context`, the website page's own
  builder:
  - tasks by phase, each with its status, points and overdue flag
  - progress and points
  - upcoming meetings, with homework and any absence request
  - meeting attendance
  - the revealed big
  - this week's study hours
  - A song task includes its lyrics, as the web page shows them. A due big
    reveal is applied first, as on the website.
- `pledge/tasks/<id>/quiz/`: `GET` returns the questions and answers, `POST`
  submits them for review. Nothing is saved if any answer is blank, and a
  quiz can't be submitted twice.
- `pledge/tasks/<id>/analysis/` is the class breakdown, but only when the chair
  has shared it. Same anonymity rules as the website (`quiz_analysis_context`).
- `POST pledge/meetings/<id>/absence/` asks to be excused from a meeting that
  hasn't happened yet.
- Every quiz route checks `pledge_may_see_task` first.
  `test_pledge_task_entitlement` now also checks that the app's quiz routes
  call it.
- Moved out of the web views in `src/view/pledge_tasks.py`, which now call
  them too: `quiz_state`, `save_quiz_answers`, `request_absence`.

#### Tests
- `src/tests/mobile/test_agendas_and_pledge_tasks.py` covers:
  - members seeing published agendas only
  - members refused on every write
  - adding, editing, moving and deleting items
  - starting minutes, and only once
  - the flag being off
  - the pledge-only gate
  - a quiz assigned to someone else
  - quiz submission and its validation
  - the analysis sharing rule
  - absence requests
  - the website's agenda and quiz forms still working through the shared
    helpers
