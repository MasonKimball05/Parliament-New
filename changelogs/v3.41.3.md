## 🟡 Preview showed a removed line as "2. a. b. d. e. f. …"

**What was wrong.** An amendment that removed one officer line (item c) from
Constitution Art. III § 3 showed in the preview as
`“2. a. b. d. e. f. g. h. i. 3. a. b. c. d. i.”`: only the list markers.

Two causes, both in `templates/cnb/resolution_print.html`:
- **Line endings.** A textarea posts CRLF; section text is stored with LF. The
  diff compares tokens *with their leading whitespace*, so the first word of
  every line ("2.", "a.", "b." …) looked changed and the rest of each line
  looked the same.
- **A Change only listed added words.** So even with a correct diff, an
  amendment that removes a line had nothing to show.

**Fix.**
- The preview's diff compares tokens by a key (the word, plus whether it
  starts a line), so CRLF/LF and indent width no longer count as changes.
  Amendments already saved with CRLF display correctly without re-saving.
- The inline callout now lists each run of changed words in order, labelled:
  **Removed: “c. …”** (struck through) and **Added: “…”**. The appendix
  shows the full section with the removed line struck through, as before.
- `add_amendment` stores the proposed text with LF. That also fixes the
  addition test (`old text in new text`), which CRLF had been defeating, so
  additions were being typed as changes.
- The live diff in the amendment editor (edit page and resolution page)
  normalizes CRLF too.

## 🟡 Amendment editor scrolled the page behind it

**What was wrong.** The popup had only a `max-height`, and its middle area
was `overflow-hidden`. With nothing inside able to scroll, the wheel scrolled
the page behind; on a short or narrow window the lower panel was clipped with
no way to reach it.

**Fix** (same popup on `resolution_form.html` and `resolution_detail.html`):
- The page behind is locked while the popup is open (every open/close path).
- The popup has a fixed height, so the text box and the tracked-changes panel
  fill it and scroll on their own.
- On a narrow window the two panels stack and the middle area scrolls.

**Checked in a real browser** (Chromium, 1400×800 and 800×600, both pages):
wheel over the editor scrolls the text box and the page behind stays put; the
Save Amendment button stays visible; the lock is released on close.
