# v3.44.2 — 10-03-26

**Committed:** *not yet*
**Deployed:** *not yet*.
**Migrations:** none.

Three fixes to the resolution work of v3.43.0 / v3.44.0.

**Found by:** the 10-03-26 auto-run review. Each was reproduced with a test
before it was fixed.

## 🟠 A struck section could take down a resolution's pages

**What was wrong.** A proposed new section stores its place as "in front of
this section". `Section.objects` hides sections a resolution has struck
(v3.44.0), but that stored link still reaches one. `plan_section_insert` then
looked for it in a list it was no longer in, with a bare `next()`:
`StopIteration`, a 500.

- **Another resolution strikes it.** Resolution A proposes a new section in
  front of § 3; resolution B passes and strikes § 3. A's resolution page, edit
  page, print preview and document preview all returned 500, for members too.
- **The same resolution strikes it.** "Strike § 2 and put a new section where
  it was" could be drafted, but its document preview returned 500 and marking
  it passed returned 500 (nothing was applied; the transaction rolled back).

**Fix.**
- `cnb_structure.close_gap`: when a section is struck, every unapplied
  proposal placed in front of it is re-pointed at the section that took its
  place, or to the end of the article if it was the last. So the new section
  lands between the same neighbours it was drafted between.
- `plan_section_insert` / `plan_article_insert`: a target that is not in the
  document raises `StructureError` (shown on the page as the proposal's
  error; a pass is refused with the message), not `StopIteration`. This
  covers a proposal already stranded before this release: edit it and choose
  its place again.

## 🟡 A resolution's status could be changed from any status

**What was wrong.** `set_resolution_status` listed its valid transitions in
the docstring and did not check them. The page only offers the right buttons,
but the POST accepted anything:

- `passed` sent again on a passed resolution (a tab left open, a second chair)
  re-applied its amendments. If a later resolution had since amended the same
  section, the older text was written back over it.
- `failed` on a passed resolution set the failure protection on its sections;
  `draft` reopened it for editing.
- `passed` on a draft skipped "pending vote".

**Fix.** `_STATUS_TRANSITIONS` (draft → pending | withdrawn; pending → passed |
failed | withdrawn | draft; passed, failed and withdrawn are final), checked
against the row read with `select_for_update`, so two "passed" posts at the
same moment cannot both apply.

## 🟡 An amendment to a struck section was "applied" to nothing

**What was wrong.** Resolution A amends the text of § 3; resolution B passes
and strikes § 3; A then passes. A's text was written into the removed row,
which no page shows, and the message said "1 section(s) updated".

**Fix.** `Resolution.apply_amendments` raises `StructureError` for a text
amendment whose section has been removed. The pass is refused with the reason
and nothing is written. `set_resolution_status` now catches the error from
both the amendments and the structural changes.
