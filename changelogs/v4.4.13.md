## A made-up chapter for App Store screenshots

App Store screenshots are public, so they can't show real members' names,
photos, phone numbers or chapter business. `seed_demo_chapter` fills a
scratch database with a fictional chapter to take them from, and to try the
app without real data:

```bash
DB_NAME=parliament_demo python manage.py migrate
DB_NAME=parliament_demo python manage.py seed_demo_chapter
```

It prints a sign-in for `demo`, the chapter Secretary (shown once; 2FA
exempt so the simulator signs straight in). The chapter has:

- 24 members with invented names, `@example.com` emails and 555-01xx
  numbers, including officers, three pledges and two committees.
- Weekly chapter meetings, study hours, a 5K with sign-ups, a dinner and a
  formal, dated from today so the calendar always has the next three weeks.
- Four announcements, chat in a chapter channel and two committee channels,
  four songs with original lyrics, three Quote Book quotes.
- A vote open now (on Home) and a passed one.

`--reset` removes it. **It refuses to run on a database with real members**
(anyone whose user id doesn't start with `DEMO-`). `--alongside` seeds next
to them, but only with DEBUG on, so never in production. Nothing is sent:
events have no GroupMe groups and their reminders are off, and announcements
don't email.

## App Review: a reviewer deleting the demo account no longer locks out the next review

Reviewers often try Settings → Delete account on the demo account. Running
`create_app_review_account` again turned it back on but left the deletion
pending, so it stayed hidden as a closed account and would have been purged
at 30 days; the next review couldn't sign in. Re-running the command now
reopens it completely and says so ("It had been deleted… it is open again").

## Slating: Close Voting stays closed

On chapter night, Close Voting "didn't stick": within a minute the election
was back to Voting Open, on round 1, with nothing decided. The background
job that follows an election's schedule flipped any election in
deliberation with a past "voting opens" time back to Voting Open, and closing
a round puts the election back in deliberation. It changed the status
directly, outside the rounds, so nothing was counted. (It ran only since the
scheduled jobs moved to the worker on 11-10; its scheduled close also marked
voting closed without counting the round.)

The job now follows the schedule exactly as the slating pages do: the first
round opens at its time through the chair's own Open, an open round closes
at its time by counting it, results publish on their date, and a round the
chair closed or paused is never reopened by the clock.

## Slating: the chapter can elect a slating committee member from the floor

Slating committee members can't run (the chapter's rule), so adding one as a
floor nominee was refused. The refusal showed only at the top of the page,
so it looked as if Add nominee did nothing. When the chapter decides to make
the exception, the chair ticks **Slating committee member (the chapter
allowed it)** next to Add nominee. Only that rule is lifted (pledges,
advisors and inactive members are still refused), and it's recorded in the
election's activity log. The refusal now says how to do this.
