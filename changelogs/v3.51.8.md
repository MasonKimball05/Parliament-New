## What was wrong

v3.51.7's changelog was written `**Committed:** *pending*`. Both
`scripts/stamp_ledger.py` and `src.W003` only recognise `not yet`,
`not committed`, `uncommitted` and `pending commit`. A line with neither a sha
nor one of those phrases is treated as prose and skipped on purpose, so the
release was pushed with its Committed line still unstamped (the `DEPLOYED.md`
row *was* stamped, as `d16202f`) and nothing warned.

## Fix

- `'pending'` added to `NOT_YET` (`scripts/stamp_ledger.py`) and `_NOT_YET`
  (`src/checks_ledger.py`). The sha-first rule is unchanged, so a line that
  names a commit is still never touched.
- `changelogs/v3.51.7.md`: Committed line stamped `10-23-26, `d16202f``.
- Tests: `*pending*` is stamped, W003 reports a committed `*pending*`
  changelog, and the two phrase lists must stay identical (they were only
  kept in sync by a comment). The two new behaviour tests fail without the
  fix.
