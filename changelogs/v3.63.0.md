## 🟢 Mobile app: Committees (Mason, 10-29-26)

The committee pages get `/api/mobile/v1/` endpoints
(`src/mobile/views_committees.py`). Who may open a committee is
`committee_home`'s rule (member, chair, advisor, VP, committee admin, site
admin); the `committee_system` flag and each web page's toggle apply.

- `GET committees/` — your committees and your role in each, as
  `committee_index` lists them (`?all=1` for site admins). Committees without
  a code are left out, since every committee page is addressed by code.
- `GET committees/<code>/` — the dashboard: your roles, people/document/open
  ballot counts, recent documents, the committee's chat channel, and links to
  what stays on the website: the education, recruitment, slating and Kai
  dashboards, and the chair tools (upload, create a vote, push to chapter,
  attendance, manage members, chat permissions) for those who can manage.
  Creates the member's `CommitteePermissions` row on first visit, as
  `committee_home` does.
- `GET committees/<code>/members/` — chairs, advisors and members, with who
  votes.
- `GET committees/<code>/documents/` and `.../documents/<id>/file/` — the
  documents `can_user_view` allows, plus unpublished minutes for members and
  above; the file streams with the Bearer token.
- `GET committees/<code>/minutes/` — the minutes list for members and above;
  published minutes carry their document.

### Deep links
`/committees/` maps to the app screen `committees`. The app routes
`/committee/<code>/` and its documents, minutes and vote pages itself.
