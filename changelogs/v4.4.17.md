## Archived elections can still be managed

The slating dashboard and the app's Slating screen listed only active
periods, so once a period was archived nothing linked to it. Its chair
couldn't get back to destroy the interview notes, as the bylaws require, or
delete the period. The pages themselves still worked; there was just no way
to reach them.

- **The slating dashboard** gains **Archived Elections**, shown to whoever ran
  or reviewed a period: each archived period with how many interviews still
  have notes, and links to **Interviews** (where the notes are destroyed),
  **Results** and, for the people running it, **Manage election** (where a
  site admin can delete it). Ordinary members don't see it.

### In the app

- `slating/` gains `archived`: each `{id, name, term, is_chair, is_committee,
  notes_remaining}`, for the archived periods this member ran or reviewed.
- The app's Slating screen lists them under **Archived elections**:
  Interviews (with how many still have notes), Results, and for the chair
  Election setup (Delete the election).
