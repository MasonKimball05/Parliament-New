## 1. `/api/admin-roles/` is admin-only

`get_admin_roles` was `@login_required` only, so any signed-in account
(pledges and advisors included) could list which roles grant admin. Its one
caller is the admin-only Sync Officer Admins modal on the member list, and
`sync_officer_admins` is admin-only too, so it now also has `@admin_required`.
Low severity (names and codes of roles, no member data), but it's a map of
which role to aim for.

## 2. `word_diff` never emits an empty `<del>` / `<ins>`

`_TOKEN.split('')` is `['']`, so a diff from or to an empty value rendered an
empty `<del>` or `<ins>` next to the real change. Cosmetic.
