## 🟠 Honeypot IP bans now expire after 24 hours

**What was wrong.** `log_and_block_honeypot_access` wrote `IPBlacklist` rows
with no `expires_at`. `tasks.expire_stale_ip_blacklist_entries` only
deactivates rows that *have* one (`.exclude(expires_at=None)`), so every
honeypot ban was **permanent** — while the cache ban was 24 h and the log line
said "IP banned for 24 hours". That is why the platform owner's IPv4 and IPv6
stayed blocked on 09-27 until the rows were deactivated by hand. On a shared
address (campus NAT, carrier CGNAT) one scanner would have locked out everyone
behind it, with no recovery.

**Fix.** Honeypot bans are created with
`expires_at = now + HONEYPOT_BAN_DURATION` (24 h); the existing Celery task
deactivates them. A repeat hit after expiry re-bans. Manual admin bans with no
expiry are unchanged (still permanent).

## 🔴 The platform owner could be locked out of the platform by the IP blacklist

(Prepared 09-27-26 as `AI/09-27-26-owner-blacklist-bypass.patch`, originally
numbered v3.35.1 — that number went to the C&B cross-reference fix.)

Mason's sentinel project probed `/.env` and `/.git/config` from his own network;
the honeypot banned his IPs, and `InputSanitizationMiddleware` then refused
every request including his logged-in browser.

**Fix.** The owner's already-authenticated session passes the blacklist gate
(`_is_owner_session`, the same single-account `user_id == '73'` pin as
`bug_admin_required`). Deliberately narrow ("Myself only"):

- Any **other** member on a blacklisted IP is still blocked.
- **Anonymous** requests from the IP — including `/login/` and the honeypot URLs
  — are still blocked. It only helps a session that already exists.
- The `IPBlacklist` row is never touched.
- Every bypassed request logs `BLACKLISTED_IP_OWNER_BYPASS` to `admin_actions`.
- The owner check runs only after an IP is known to be blacklisted, so normal
  requests pay nothing.

**Trade-off, accepted:** a stolen owner session is not stopped by the IP
blacklist. 2FA, quarantine and lockdown all still apply.

**⚠️ `multi-chapter` merge note.** That branch forbids the `'73'` literal
(`test_platform_owner.py`). When main is merged in, `_is_owner_session`'s body
becomes `return is_platform_owner(getattr(request, 'user', None))`.
