## 🟡 `make stamp-ledger` couldn't clear the W003 it was asked to fix

**Found by:** the 10-01-26 auto-run review.

**What was wrong.** `src.W003` reads a changelog's commit line as
`**Committed:**` *or* `**Committed & pushed:**`. `scripts/stamp_ledger.py`
only matched the long spelling. v3.37.1 and v3.38.0 were written with the
short one, so after they landed on `main`:

- `src.W003` fired: "v3.37.1 (committed in b685bc7), v3.38.0 (committed in
  4100131)" still say *not yet*.
- `make stamp-ledger` and the pre-push hook's auto-stamp both said "Nothing to
  do".
- So the next hooked push would be refused, and the one command the hook
  recommends couldn't fix it.

**Fix.**
- `stamp_ledger.py` uses the same pattern as `checks_ledger._COMMITTED_LINE`
  and keeps whichever spelling the line was written with.
- The two changelogs are stamped.
- 4 tests in `src/tests/infra/test_stamp_ledger.py`
  (`StampLedgerReadsEveryCommittedSpellingTests`), including one that the tool
  and the check accept the same spellings. They fail without the fix.

## Deploy

Nothing to deploy: the change is in the dev-side ledger tooling and two
