## Resolutions are open to every brother

"Update the Resolutions page so anyone can use or make a resolution." His
calls: **brothers only** (Members, Chairs, Officers, admins; not pledges or
advisors), and the author gets **full control of his own resolution except
Passed / Failed**. Those two stay with the C&B Chair because Passed applies the
amendments and new sections to the live Constitution & Bylaws on the spot, and
Failed locks the affected sections from amendment.

- New `ParliamentUser.can_create_resolution` and
  `cnb._can_manage_resolution(user, resolution)` (chair, or the author while he
  is still a brother).
- **Create:** `cnb_create_resolution` no longer `@cnb_required`. "New Resolution"
  shows on the C&B hub and the resolutions list for brothers. Pledges and
  advisors still read everything, as before.
- **Author:** edits the text, amendments and structure changes (non-chairs
  still only see enabled documents, as editors always did). He can also add or
  remove collaborators (brothers only, while the chair can still add anyone),
  move Draft ↔ Pending, Withdraw, and use and clear the working-group notes.
- **Passed / Failed:** chair only, enforced in `set_resolution_status`
  (`_AUTHOR_STATUSES`). The author's Actions box says the chair records the
  result.
- Tests: `src/tests/legislation/test_resolutions_open_to_brothers.py` (15).
  `test_cnb_document_pdf`'s banner test now expects "New Resolution" for a
  Member.

## Chairs can't hand out roles or promote people (10-18 #needs-review)

"Only someone with officers should be able to give others admin." Confirmed
as: chairs lose role assignment, transfer and role editing, and can't change
member types past Member (or their own). Officers keep all of it, and
admin-granting roles stay admin-only (v3.50.1).

- `role_change_error`: a non-officer (chair) gets "Only officers can give or
  take roles." That covers `assign_role_member`, `unassign_role_member`,
  `transfer_role`, `edit_member` roles, `add_member` roles and
  `assign_appointment`.
- `add_role`, editing a role (`role_detail` POST) and `delete_role` are
  officers-only. A chair could otherwise rename the code of a role he holds to
  one that carries permissions (e.g. `CNB`).
- New `member_type_change_error`: a chair can only set Member or Pledge, can't
  touch an Officer, Chair or Advisor's type, and can't change his own. It's
  enforced in `edit_member`, `add_member` and bulk import (per row).
- Tests: `src/tests/users/test_chair_role_limits.py` (11 of 15).

## Appointment votes: assigned only after they pass, and only once

`assign_appointment` now refuses (GET and POST) unless the vote is closed and
passed and not already assigned. The "assigned" flag is claimed under a row
lock before the role is added, so a double-submit or two officers can't both
assign it. Tests: 4 more in `test_chair_role_limits.py`.

## GroupMe: "N posts waiting more than 10 minutes"

have shown v3.49.0's Postgres bug on the first announcement. Tests:
`src/tests/officer/test_groupme_stuck.py` (3).
