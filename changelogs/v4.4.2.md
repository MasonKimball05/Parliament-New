The app gets native slating applications and committee tools, service-hours
setup, member and songbook editing, chapter document management, the bug

## The app: slating

Members apply for a position and see their applications in the app
(`src/mobile/views_slating_apply.py`); the form draws every field type the form
builder has, with photos for file fields. The slating committee reviews
applications and runs interviews, and the period's chair takes attendance,
records paper ballots, and reads and publishes the results summary
(`src/mobile/views_slating_committee.py`). Each uses the website's own code,
moved into helpers in `src/view/slating/` (`save_application`, `save_review`,
`save_schedule`, `mark_attendance`, `save_paper_ballots`,
`publish_period_results`, …), so the logs, the Chapter Documents filing and
the publish notification are the same.

The slate builder, period setup, positions, the form builder, import and
transfer-admin stay on the website.

**Tests:** `mobile.test_slating_batch5`.

## The app: service-hours setup

The VP of Philanthropy manages service periods, member expectations and
service events (create, edit, attendance, finalize) in the app
(`src/mobile/views_service_admin.py`), and members open their own submissions
natively. The form builder, hour adjustments and the CSV export stay on the
website.

Website fixes from the shared helpers in `src/view/service_hours.py`:
- The service event edit page crashed on every load; it opens again.
- Deleting a period that still has events, adding a duplicate expectation, or
  entering junk hours showed a server error; each now shows a message.
- Reminder hours are kept to 1–168 and event hours to at most 24.
- Hours from a finalized event are credited on the chapter's date, not UTC's
  (a 9 PM event was credited to the next day).
- Finalizing locks the event row, so two finalizes at once can't credit the
  hours twice.

**Tests:** `mobile.test_service_hours_batch5`.

## The app: members, songbook and chapter documents

Officers add a member (the temporary password shows once, as on the website),
edit a member and initiate pledges (`src/mobile/views_member_admin.py`). Those
who manage songs add, edit and remove them, and admins manage categories and
Choristers (`views_songbook_manage.py`). Officers manage chapter documents and
admins their folders (`views_document_manage.py`). Uploading a document and a
song's recording stay on the website until the app has a file picker.

Website changes: a document title over 200 characters, an unknown document
type, or a folder name over 100 characters is refused with a message instead
of failing in the database, and a malformed initiate-pledges request is a 400
instead of a server error.

**Tests:** `mobile.test_officer_tools_batch5`.

## The app: chapter votes and legislation

Authors, chairs and officers run chapter votes in the app
(`src/mobile/views_legislation_manage.py`): put a bill or a chair appointment
on the ballot, open it now, end it, call a runoff, reopen, quick attendance,
edit a bill, submit a new version, add to the tracker, officer notes and
assigning an appointment's role. Members check a vote receipt. Everything goes
through `src/chapter_votes.py`, which the website's views now call too, so
outcomes, broadcasts, notifications and logs are the same. Attaching a PDF or
Word document, deleting a bill and editing a poll stay on the website.

Website changes:
- Ending a vote that has already ended is refused. A second End Vote (a double
  tap, a resent form) used to close it again and send every voter the result
  again.
- A junk number or role id on the upload forms shows a message instead of a
  server error.

**Tests:** `mobile.test_legislation_batch6`; `legislation.test_voting`
(`test_a_closed_vote_is_not_ended_again`).

## The app: Kai review, committee settings, chat channels

The Kai committee reviews cases in the app (`src/mobile/views_kai_review.py`):
the list with its filters and bulk actions, each case and its actions,
recusals, stand-ins, report templates and the committee's member
permissions. A recused member (the accused, or anyone recused by hand) gets no
case details. Print, the CSV export, the form builders and attachments stay on
the website.

Committee chairs edit guest chat permissions, chat settings and recruitment
permissions, and officers create, edit and delete committees
(`views_committee_settings.py`). Admins create and edit chat channels
(`views_chat_channels.py`).

Website fixes: saving a recruitment member's permissions crashed every time;
it saves again. A bad chat icon or colour, or a committee name that's taken,
shows a message instead of a server error.

Two names on the Kai case page went past the identity flags, on the website
and (copied) in the app; both are fixed in `party_label`
(`src/view/kai_reports.py`):
- A closure or drop request said "Requested by <name>" to anyone who could
  close cases. It now says "The submitter" or "The accused" unless the viewer
  may see that identity.
- The recused-seats panel named the seat holder, who is the accused when the
  accused sits on the committee. Same rule.
- A stand-in's dashboard said whose seat they fill; for a party's seat it now
  says "a recused member".

`docs/CONFIDENTIALITY_MATRIX.md` lists the surfaces.

**Tests:** `mobile.test_kai_review_batch6` (incl.
`test_party_names_follow_the_identity_flags`,
`test_a_stand_in_is_not_told_whose_seat_it_is`) and the committee settings and
chat channel tests alongside it.

## The app: C&B editing, change password, browser sessions, sign-ups

Those who manage the Constitution and Bylaws add and retitle articles, add
sections, edit their text and suspend or reinstate them
(`src/mobile/views_cnb_manage.py`); every change goes through
`Section.record_revision`, so section history stays right. The resolution
working group keeps its notes in the app. Members change their password (this
phone stays signed in; every other session is signed out, as on the website)
and see and end their browser sessions (`views_account_security.py`).
Officers see an event's sign-up list. On the website, a section or article
number over 20 characters or a title over 200 shows a message instead of a
database error.

**Tests:** `mobile.test_cnb_minutes_account_batch6`.

## Polish

- Officer tools are grouped (Communications, Events and attendance, Votes and
  records, Members and roles, Programs) and searchable.
- Saving shows a short confirmation, and leaving a long form with unsaved
  typing asks first.
- Filters above a list are one row that scrolls sideways instead of filling
  the screen (bug tracker, feedback, Kai, slating, All Activity).
- Dates outside this year show the year.
- A "not allowed" or "not found" error no longer offers Try again, and screens
  no longer show their buttons or filters above one.
- The feedback form attaches a photo (`attachment` on `support/feedback/`,
  checked like a bug report's screenshot); PDFs stay on the website.
- Small fixes: no empty pill on a bug report, no "Chorister · Chorister", the
  service pages' permission message fits every page.

**Tests:** `mobile.test_support` (feedback photo).
