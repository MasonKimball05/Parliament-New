## 🟢 Slating changes (Mason, 10-26-26)

### Voting history is kept, and the export includes it
Every voting round keeps its number, its raw votes and a results snapshot
(new `SlatingRound`).
- **Reopening after a failed round starts a new round.** Before, any reopen
  from deliberation counted as resuming a pause, so a failed slate round's
  ballots could block members from voting again. A pause still resumes the
  same round.
- **Individual re-votes no longer delete** the failed positions' earlier
  votes. Counts are per round now, and failed positions go back on the ballot
  as pending.
- **"Clear & Reset Votes" is now "Void This Round".** The round stays in the
  history marked *voided* and isn't counted; reopening starts a new round.
- **A candidate replaced mid-round** has their counts saved to the round
  history first. Deleting the candidate removes their votes.
- **The results export** ("Election Results" in Chapter Documents) is built
  from the round history: the final round's result, then a **Voting history**
  section with every round (when it opened and closed, outcome, and
  online/paper counts), voided rounds and candidate replacements included.
  The results summary and the results page show the same history. *Fixed in
  passing:* the results page and the export counted every round's slate votes
  together, so a re-vote's official numbers included the round that failed.

### Paper ballots
For members who voted on paper because of tech problems. **Paper Ballots**
(next to Pause/Close Voting, and on the paused-vote panel) takes this round's
paper counts:
- **Full-slate vote:** approve, reject and abstain.
- **Individual vote:** the number of ballots, then each position's counts. No
  position can have more votes than there were ballots.

They can be entered while a round is open or paused, and saving again replaces
them. Closing the vote adds them to the online votes. Results, the summary
and the export show them separately. Each save is logged with counts only
(`SlatingPaperBallots`; no voter identity, like an online vote).

### Results stay hidden until the committee announces them
Members no longer see a running tally after voting. The individual-vote page
no longer marks earlier positions Passed/Failed; they're "decided in an
earlier round". The results page was already gated on publishing, and
`period_status` shows tallies only to managers (v3.57.0).

### No IPs in slating
Slating activity records no IP address. The views stopped passing one, the
migration cleared the stored values (nginx's socket address anyway), and the
admin column is hidden. This closes `test_client_ip_single_source`'s
`PENDING_DECISION`, which is now empty.

### Who can apply
Only **active members**: inactive members, alumni and advisors can't apply.
The apply page refuses them, the eligibility check says why, and Import
Applications skips them, all through one rule
(`permissions.applicant_ineligibility`). Before, the apply page checked
nothing, so inactive members and alumni could apply.

### Import: one form per position
The President's Google Form took one submission per position, so a member
running for three positions has three rows. Import Applications now combines
each member's rows into one application instead of keeping only the latest:
- every position is kept, each in the highest tier any form put it in;
- a newer form for the **same** position replaces the older one (a
  correction);
- text answers that differ are kept side by side, labelled by position
  ("President: … / Treasurer: …"); identical answers are kept once;
  multi-select answers are joined; other answers and GPA keep the latest,
  with a warning when forms disagreed;
- unmapped answers go to the notes tagged with their position, and the
  application is dated by the member's first form.

The confirmation screen shows "combined from rows 2, 4, 5", and each folded-in
row says which application it went into. The review notes record every row
used. Tests in `OneFormPerPositionTests` (3).

### Pledges
- **Can apply** for slating, as far as Parliament's rules go. The eligibility
  check no longer refuses them. ⚠️ The **Slating — Apply** page is also behind a
  VPE pledge-page restriction that migration `0002` seeds as *blocked for all
  phases*. Pledges can actually apply once the VPE opens that page on the
  education dashboard's pledge page access. That setting wasn't changed here.
- **Must be initiated before being interviewed.** Scheduling a pledge's
  interview is refused with that message, and the Interviews page shows
  "Pledge — interview after initiation".
- **Must be initiated before serving on the slating committee.** A pledge
  listed on the committee gets no committee or manager access, and isn't
  offered as an interviewer.

### Destroy interview notes button
The Interviews page has a **Destroy interview notes** section for the
period's managers. It explains what's erased (notes, strengths, concerns,
kept as the bylaws require once the minutes are approved) and needs
**DESTROY** typed. The view existed, but no page linked to it.
