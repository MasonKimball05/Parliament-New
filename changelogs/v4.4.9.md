## Education: one pledge class page per semester, held-back pledges included

v4.4.8 started saving a pledge class when it was initiated. Pledges
initiated within a week of each other shared a page, which didn't fit the
chapter's practice of holding a pledge back a semester when he isn't ready.
Now:

- **One page per semester.** Each class page is the semester of initiation
  (Spring is January to June, Fall is July to December, the same split as
  the pledge-class badges), named from that semester's class, e.g. "Theta
  class (Fall 2026)". Everyone initiated that semester goes on it, whenever
  they're initiated: the class, a straggler a few days later, and a pledge
  held back from an earlier semester.
- **A held-back pledge keeps his whole pledgeship.** Meetings from his
  first semester that he was on the roster for still count in his points
  and show in the class's meeting list. On the page his row says when he
  pledged ("Pledged Spring 2026").
- **On the live dashboard too.** While he's still pledging, his earlier
  meetings count for him but not for the new pledges, who weren't there.
  Before, once a class was saved, an earlier meeting either counted for
  everyone or for no one.

## Education: the class initiated on 11-08 can be rebuilt

That class was initiated before classes were saved, so it has no page. Their
records are all still in the database (initiation keeps each member's
account), checked on the server: 7 members, 175 task results, 278 quiz
answers, 31 attendance records, 1 point adjustment and 7 bigs, with no tasks
added since.

`python manage.py backfill_pledge_class` rebuilds a page for every
initiation in the activity log that doesn't have one. It shows the
dashboard as it stood at that moment: only tasks that existed then, meetings
held and point adjustments made by then. `--dry-run` shows each member's
points and tasks without saving anything. Running it twice does nothing the
second time. The page's edit history says it was rebuilt afterwards.
