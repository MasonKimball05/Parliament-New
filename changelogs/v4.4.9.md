## Education: one pledge class page per semester, held-back pledges included

v4.4.8 started saving a pledge class when it was initiated. Pledges
initiated within a week of each other shared a page, which didn't fit the
chapter's practice of holding a pledge back a semester when he isn't ready.
Now:

- **One page per semester.** Each class page is the semester of initiation
  (Spring is January to June, Fall is July to December, the same split as
  the pledge-class badges), named from that semester's class, e.g. "Theta
  class (Fall 2026)". Everyone initiated that semester goes on it, whenever
  they're initiated: the class, a straggler a few days later, and a pledge
  held back from an earlier semester.
- **A held-back pledge keeps his whole pledgeship.** Meetings from his
  first semester that he was on the roster for still count in his points
  and show in the class's meeting list. On the page his row says when he
  pledged ("Pledged Spring 2026").
- **On the live dashboard too.** While he's still pledging, his earlier
  meetings count for him but not for the new pledges, who weren't there.
  Before, once a class was saved, an earlier meeting either counted for
  everyone or for no one.

## Education: the class initiated on 11-08 can be rebuilt

That class was initiated before classes were saved, so it has no page. Their
records are all still in the database (initiation keeps each member's
account), checked on the server: 7 members, 175 task results, 278 quiz
answers, 31 attendance records, 1 point adjustment and 7 bigs, with no tasks
added since.

`python manage.py backfill_pledge_class` rebuilds a page for every
initiation in the activity log that doesn't have one. It shows the
dashboard as it stood at that moment: only tasks that existed then, meetings
held and point adjustments made by then. `--dry-run` shows each member's
points and tasks without saving anything. Running it twice does nothing the
second time. The page's edit history says it was rebuilt afterwards.

## Education: held-back pledges, re-required tasks and the VP of Education

From Mason's answers on pledge class edge cases:

- **The VP of Education has the education admin's access.** When the
  committee has one, whoever holds its role (the VP of Education) can do
  everything a chair can on the education pages, including correcting class
  pages, whether or not he's listed as a chair.
- **Held-back pledges are recorded every semester.** Once a new semester
  starts, a daily job puts each pledge who was already pledging last
  semester on that semester's class page under **Held Back**: his points,
  required tasks, task results and attendance as they stood when the
  semester ended. If nobody was initiated that semester, the page is created
  ("No one was initiated this semester"). Combined with his entry on the page
  of the semester he's finally initiated in, every semester of his
  pledgeship is kept.
- **Optional points reset.** A new setting on the dashboard's Previous Pledge
  Classes card (education admin only): "Reset a held-back pledge's points at
  the start of each semester". When it's on, the job also starts his points
  again at zero. It does that with one point adjustment ("Semester reset:
  reached 52/60 in Spring 2026"), which shows in his adjustment history and
  can be deleted to undo it. Off by default.
- **Re-require tasks.** On a pledge's own page, anyone who can manage tasks
  can **Re-require** a task, or **Re-require all tasks** he has a result for.
  His earlier result, score, notes and a quiz's answers are kept as an
  earlier attempt, shown under the task ("Earlier (Spring 2026):
  Completed · 9/10"), and the task is open again for him. If his points were
  reset after he did it, the task is taken back out of the reset, so he can
  earn it again rather than being docked for redoing it.
- **Removing someone from a class page.** The education admin can remove a
  member from a class page (an initiation made by mistake). His record moves
  to a **Removed** list on the page and can be restored. Both are logged.
- **Pledges become members through Initiate Pledges.** Changing a pledge's
  member type in Admin v2's member editor or the officers' Edit Member is now
  refused, with a pointer to Initiate Pledges, which assigns his roll number
  and saves his class. To do it anyway, tick the new **Initiate anyway** box
  (website only); his class page entry is still saved. Admin v2's bulk
  change-type already refused pledges.

Dropped pledges need nothing new: deleting a pledge's account removes his
live records, and class pages keep their own copy.
