## 🟢 Mobile app: more officer pages (Mason, 10-30-26)

`src/mobile/views_officer_tools.py`. Each one returns the website page's own
data with the same access rules.

- `officer/attendance-events/` mirrors `event_attendance_list`: upcoming events
  and the last 20 past ones, with their counts.
- `officer/stats/?days=` mirrors `chapter_stats`. The calculation is now
  `chapter_stats_context`, shared by the website page and the app.
- `officer/members/` mirrors `user_list`: everyone who isn't removed, with
  status, contact, roles and last sign-in.
- `officer/contact/`, plus `<id>/read/` and `read-all/`, mirror
  `contact_submissions` (officers and advisors).
- `officer/reports/` and `<id>/file/` mirror `view_all_reports`: every committee
  document, published or not (officers and advisors).
- `officer/` gains `unread_contact`.
- Tests are in `src/tests/mobile/test_officer.py`.
