## 🟢 Education dashboard: when each pledge was last on the site

Mason, 10-16-26: "an indicator to the education dashboard that shows when
the last time a pledge logged onto the site … Not just entered credentials,
the last time they got on."

### Why not `last_login`
`last_login` only moves when a password is typed. A pledge on a remembered
(30-day) session can use the site every day and still show a `last_login`
from weeks ago.

### What changed
- **New field `ParliamentUser.last_seen_at`** (nullable). `SessionTrackingMiddleware`
  sets it on signed-in requests, inside its existing 5-minute-per-session
  throttle, so it's one extra `UPDATE` per session every 5 minutes, not per
  request. Accurate to within 5 minutes.
- **An admin's "log in as" doesn't count.** That's the admin on the site, not
  the member (`is_impersonating`).
- **Backfill:** the migration starts everyone from the newer of their
  `last_login` and their newest tracked session's `last_activity`, so the
  dashboard isn't all "Never on" on day one. (Caveat: `last_login` can include
  a past admin "log in as", since `login()` sets it; this only affects the
  starting value.)
- **Dashboard → Pledge Progress:** each row shows *Last on today / yesterday /
  N days ago* (hover for the exact time). Grey under 7 days, **amber** at 7+,
  **red** at 14+ or **Never on**.
- Shown to anyone who can open the education dashboard.
- `last_seen_at` added to `MEMBER_ACCOUNT_FIELDS` (deferred on joins) and to
  `ANONYMIZE_FIELDS_KEPT` (same reasoning as `last_login`).
