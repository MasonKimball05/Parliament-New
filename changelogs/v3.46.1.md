## 🟢 Chair-side numbers ignored the pledge's phase

v3.46.0 hides a task from a pledge until his phase unlocks it, on My Tasks, the
quiz pages and the chair's "View as pledge" preview. Two chair-side numbers
still counted locked tasks:

- **Education dashboard, Pledge Progress, "pts".** The max is documented as
  "only what a pledge could actually have earned by now", and as agreeing with
  the pledge's own page. A live Phase 2 task counted toward a Phase 1 pledge's
  max, so every Phase 1 pledge looked behind by every later-phase task's points.
  **Now:** max and earned both count only tasks unlocked for that pledge.
- **Pledge detail page, overdue.** A Phase 2 task past its due date was listed
  as overdue for a Phase 1 pledge who cannot open it. **Now:** a locked task is
  never overdue. It still shows in the task list.

**Not changed (a question, not a bug):** the "X/Y tasks" count and the progress
bar on the dashboard still count every required task, all phases. That reads as
"progress toward initiation", which may be what you want. If you would rather it
mean "this phase", it is the `applicable_required` list in `education_home`.

## 🟢 Cancelling a confirm() left the button stuck for 10 seconds

Every form that asks "Are you sure?" (the new "Advance all pledges", the Bigs
buttons, `js-confirm-submit` forms: Kai, documents, legislation, landing page,
members) had the same bug. Answering **Cancel** stopped the POST, but the global
double-submit guard had already marked the form as submitted and put the button
into its spinner ("Advance all pledges..."), disabled, for 10 seconds. Checked in
Chromium.

**Fix (`static/js/base/double-submit.js`):**
- The guard skips a confirm form (`js-confirm-submit`, `data-confirm-msg`, or a
  pressed button with `data-confirm`) whose submit was prevented.
- It listens on `window` instead of `document`, so it runs after every form- and
  document-level handler regardless of script order.
- Every other form is guarded exactly as before. In particular AJAX forms (which
  call `preventDefault()` themselves) and `core.js`'s CSRF resubmit (which calls
  it for file-upload forms) still get the guard.

Checked in Chromium with the real file: cancel leaves the button enabled; accept
submits; an AJAX form is still locked as before.

**Known limit:** a confirm form that *also* has a file input is skipped by the
guard even when the member clicks OK, because `core.js` has already called
`preventDefault()`. No such form exists today.
