## 🟡 The query-budget guards went red with v3.47.0

v3.47.0 was checked with targeted tests only, and `src.tests.guards.test_query_budgets`
was not among them. Its new `last_seen_at` UPDATE in `SessionTrackingMiddleware`
runs on the cold path that every budget measures (the suite clears the cache
first, on purpose), so **15 of the 16 page budgets fail by exactly one query**
on `main` (e.g. `home now costs 48 queries, budget is 47`). Checked on clean
checkouts: `b7dd062` passes, `2f48848` (v3.47.0) and `18d2486` fail.

The query is justified (one UPDATE per session per 5 minutes, zero on the warm
path), so the fix is what the guard's own message asks for: each of the 15
budgets goes up by 1 with a comment saying why. No production code changes.

## 🟢 "Last on today" counted 24-hour blocks, not days

`(now - last_seen_at).days` counts whole 24-hour periods, so a pledge who was on
at 11 PM and is looked at by the chair at 8 AM showed **"Last on today"**, and
"N days ago" (and the amber/red thresholds) ran up to a day short. It now
compares calendar dates in the site's time zone (`timezone.localdate()` vs
`localtime(last_seen_at).date()`).
