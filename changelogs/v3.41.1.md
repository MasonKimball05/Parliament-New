## 🟡 Editor collaborators can actually edit a resolution

**Reported by:** Mason, 10-02-26. "The edit resolution page required
constitution and bylaws chair."

**What was wrong.** A collaborator added to a resolution as **Editor** saw the
Edit button and the amendment forms on the resolution page, because
`resolution_detail` computes `can_edit` as "C&B permission or editor
collaborator". But the views behind those controls were all `@cnb_required`:
`edit_resolution`, `add_amendment`, `remove_amendment`, and
`section_context_api` (which the amendment modal calls). So an editor who
clicked Edit was sent home with "Constitution & Bylaws Chair access required."
The Editor role granted nothing.

**Decided (Mason).** The chair and the resolution's editor collaborators can
edit the text and add/remove amendments, while it is draft or pending.
Changing status and managing collaborators stay with the chair.

**Fix** (`src/view/officer/cnb.py`):
- `_can_edit_resolution(user, resolution)`: C&B permission, or an Editor
  collaborator on **that** resolution. `edit_resolution`, `add_amendment` and
  `remove_amendment` check it in place of `@cnb_required`.
- `section_context_api`: the chair gets every section, as before. An editor of
  an open resolution gets sections of the documents members can already read,
  and nothing else, so it can't be used to read a switched-off document (the
  unpassed Foreword).
- `add_amendment`: an editor can't amend a section of a switched-off document.
- **Unchanged, still chair only:** `set_resolution_status`, `add_collaborator`,
  `remove_collaborator`, `create_resolution`.
- `resolution_detail.html`: the status Actions card was shown to editors, whose
  clicks then bounced. It is now shown to the chair only.

**Tests:** `src/tests/legislation/test_cnb_editor_collaborators.py` (13):
editor can open/save/amend; viewer collaborators, plain members and editors of
a different resolution cannot; closed resolutions stay closed; status and
collaborators stay chair-only. Four fail without the fix.
