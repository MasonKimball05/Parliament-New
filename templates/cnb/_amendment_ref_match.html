{% comment %} v3.45.3 — how a page recognises an amendment's [ref] in the resolution text. Mason, 10-13-26: "the tracked amendment refs are randomly breaking. The text for the ref is still there, but the system no longer tracks/realizes that the ref is there." A ref reads "[Bylaws Art. II § 3 (Addition) — scope - 2]". Until v3.45.3 both the edit page and the print preview looked for that exact string. Three parts of it change after the ref is pasted: - the number at the end is the amendment's place in the list, and the list is sorted by section, so adding or removing an amendment on an earlier section renumbers every one after it; - "(Addition)" / "(Deletion)" is worked out again from the text each time the amendment is saved; - the scope note can be edited. Any of those left the old ref in the text but no longer matched. A resolution has at most one amendment per section (unique_together), so the section is enough to say which amendment a ref means. A ref matches its amendment when it is "[" + the section + at least one of the amendment parts ("(Type)", "— scope", "- N") + "]". A plain citation "[Bylaws Art. II § 3]" with nothing after the section is still a citation, not a ref. Include once per page, before the scripts that use it. {% endcomment %}
