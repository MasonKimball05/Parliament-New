Anonymizing scrubs a member's name, username, email, and other contact/ profile info, replacing them with a placeholder. It does not delete the row — votes, ballots, slating records, meeting minutes, documents they authored, and everything else with a foreign key to them stay exactly as they are. Their login credentials (passkeys, 2FA devices, API tokens, push subscriptions, calendar feed) are deleted outright, and the account is deactivated.

This cannot be undone — the original name/email/etc. are not stored anywhere once this runs.
