    1. Upload the responses

    • Google Forms: open the form → Responses → ⋮ → Download responses (.csv).
    • Google Sheets / Excel: File → Download (or Save As) → Comma-separated values (.csv).
    • The first row must be the questions. Include a column that identifies each person — their email works best (turn on Collect email addresses in Google Forms).
    • If people sent one form per position, choose all the CSVs at once (or add more on the next step): questions with the same wording line up, and each member's forms are combined into one application with every position they applied for.

    Nothing is saved until you review a preview. Members who already applied in Parliament are skipped, so their own application is never overwritten.
