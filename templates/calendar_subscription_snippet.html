📚 How to subscribe

Google Calendar:

Click "Subscribe in Google Calendar" or copy the link → Settings → Add calendar → From URL → Paste link

Apple Calendar (iPhone/Mac):

Click the webcal:// link → Calendar app will open and ask to subscribe

Outlook:

Copy the link → Calendar → Add Calendar → Subscribe from web → Paste link
