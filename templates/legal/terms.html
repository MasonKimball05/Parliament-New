Parliament is the members' website and iPhone app of the Alpha Mu Chapter of Beta Theta Pi at Samford University. By using it you agree to these terms, the chapter's Constitution and Bylaws, and our Privacy Policy.

Your account

  • Accounts are for chapter members and are issued by chapter officers. Use only your own account and keep your password and devices secure.
  • Chapter business in Parliament, including votes, minutes and Kai matters, is confidential to the chapter. Don't share it outside the chapter except as the bylaws allow.

Content you post

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive members. Don't post anything that harasses, threatens, bullies or demeans anyone; hate speech; sexual content; content that promotes violence, hazing or illegal activity; spam; or anything that shares another person's private information without their permission.

  • You can report any chat message or quote.
  • Officers review reports promptly (we aim for within 24 hours), remove content that breaks these terms, and may suspend or remove the account of anyone who posts it, in addition to any action under the chapter's bylaws.
  • You're responsible for what you post. The chapter may keep it as part of its records, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Chat messages, quotes, comments, feedback and other things members post are the views of the member who posted them, not of the chapter or Beta Theta Pi, and the chapter doesn't endorse them or check them before they appear. The chapter speaks only through its official business: announcements posted by officers, adopted legislation and resolutions, approved minutes, and the Constitution and Bylaws.

Using Parliament fairly

  • Don't try to get into another member's account, or into parts of Parliament your role doesn't give you.
  • Don't copy, export or screenshot other members' contact details or confidential chapter business to share outside the chapter.
  • Don't interfere with Parliament: no automated scraping, overloading it, or probing it for weaknesses. If you find a security problem, report it through the support page.

Ending your use

You can delete your account at any time (see the Privacy Policy). Officers may suspend or close accounts of people who are no longer members or who break these terms.
