Parliament support

Parliament is the members' website and iPhone app of the Alpha Mu Chapter of Beta Theta Pi at Samford University. Here's help with the common questions; for anything else, send us a message below.

Getting an account

Accounts are for chapter members and are set up by chapter officers. If you're a member without one, ask an officer, or send a message below.

Signing in

  • Forgot your password? Reset it here.
  • Lost your two-factor device? Use one of your backup codes, or ask an officer to help you recover your account.
  • In the app you can sign in with a passkey, and turn on Face ID in Settings to unlock it.

Meeting check-in

Scan the code shown at the meeting from the app's Check in screen, or turn on location check-in in the app's Settings to be checked in when you arrive. Location check-in only works inside the check-in area while check-in is open.

Reporting a problem

Signed-in members can use Report a bug or Feedback from the app's Home screen or the website, which reaches the developers directly.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. What's deleted and what the chapter keeps is explained in the privacy policy.

Contact us

We use what you send only to reply to you. See the privacy policy.
