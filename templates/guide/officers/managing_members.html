Managing Members

Learn how to add, edit, and manage chapter members

Quick Actions

Member List

1 Adding New Members

Navigate to Officers → Members and click the "Add Member" button in the top right.

Required Information

  • Name: Full legal name of the member
  • Member ID: Unique identifier (e.g., student ID or pledge class number)
  • Member Type: Pledge, Member, or Alumni
  • Status: Active, Inactive, etc.
  • Email: Optional but recommended for notifications

Auto-generated credentials: When you add a member, their username is automatically generated from their name (first initial + last name). For pledges, the password is set to their username. For other members, a random password is generated.

2 Understanding Member Types

Pledge

New members in the pledging process. They have limited access and cannot vote on legislation.

Member

Full initiated members with voting rights. Can access all member features and participate in chapter governance.

Alumni

Graduated members. Typically have read-only access but cannot vote or hold officer positions.

3 Editing Member Information

Click on any member in the list to open their profile. Click the "Edit" button to modify their details.

Editable Fields

  • Name & Preferred Name: Update the member's display name
  • Email: Update contact email for notifications
  • Member Type & Status: Change membership classification
  • Role Number: Assigned when a pledge is initiated
  • Roles: Assign officer positions or special roles

4 Assigning Roles

Roles grant special permissions and responsibilities. When editing a member, you can assign one or more roles from the available list.

Note: Common roles include President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, and committee chairs. Some roles grant officer-level access to manage chapter features.

5 Initiating Pledges

When pledges complete their pledging process, use the batch initiation feature to convert them to full members.

How to Initiate Pledges

  1. Go to Officers → Members
  2. Click the "Initiate Pledges" button
  3. Select the pledges you want to initiate
  4. Assign a unique role number to each pledge
  5. Click "Initiate Selected"

What happens: Their member type changes from "Pledge" to "Member", they receive their role number, and they gain full voting rights. A notification is sent welcoming them to the chapter.

Important: Role numbers must be unique. The system will prevent duplicate role numbers and validate all assignments before processing.

6 Deactivating or Removing Members

Members who leave the chapter or become inactive can be removed from the system.

Soft Delete (Recommended)

Sets the member to "Inactive" status. Their account is disabled but records are preserved for historical purposes.

Hard Delete

Permanently removes the member and all their related records. This action cannot be undone.

Note: You cannot delete admin accounts or your own account. Contact a system administrator if needed.
