Attendance Management

Track event attendance and manage excuses

Quick Actions

Take Attendance Review Excuses

1 Taking Attendance

To take attendance for an event, go to Officers → Event Attendance and select the event you want to record attendance for.

Marking Attendance

For each member, you can mark their attendance status:

Present
Absent
Excused
Late

Tip: You can use the "Mark All Present" button to quickly mark everyone as present, then adjust individual entries as needed.

2 Handling Excuse Requests

Members can submit excuse requests before events they cannot attend. Officers review these requests and approve or deny them.

Review Request

Read the member's reason for requesting an excuse

Approve

Mark the member as excused for the event

Deny

Reject the excuse (member will need to attend or be marked absent)

Note: Members are notified when their excuse request is approved or denied. Be consistent with excuse policies to ensure fairness.

3 Viewing Attendance Reports

You can view attendance reports to see trends and identify members who may need follow-up.

Available Reports

  • Event Summary: See attendance breakdown for a specific event
  • Member History: View a member's attendance record over time
  • Chapter Overview: Overall attendance statistics for the chapter

Best Practices

  • 1 Take attendance promptly - Record attendance during or immediately after the event for accuracy
  • 2 Review excuses regularly - Don't let excuse requests pile up; review them before the event when possible
  • 3 Be consistent - Apply the same standards to all excuse requests to ensure fairness
  • 4 Follow up on absences - Reach out to members with multiple unexcused absences
