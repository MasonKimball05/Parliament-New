Attendance Management

Track event attendance and manage excuses

Quick Actions

Take Attendance Review Excuses

1 Where to Take Attendance

Attendance can be taken from three different places. All three sync to the same underlying records — marking someone present in one will reflect everywhere else.

Attendance Page (primary)

Go to Officers → Event Attendance and select an event. Shows all active members with toggle buttons. Best for taking attendance before or after a meeting.

Chapter Minutes Editor

The Attendance section inside the minutes editor. If the minutes are linked to an event, changes here sync to that event's records. Convenient for recording attendance while writing meeting notes.

Vote Page — Attendance Tab

The officer panel on the live Vote page has an Attendance tab. Lets you mark attendance without leaving the vote page — useful during chapter meetings when a vote is open.

2 Attendance Statuses

For each member, mark one of five statuses:

Present
Attended
Late
Arrived late but attended
Absent
Did not attend
Excused
Approved excuse on file
Pending
Not yet marked

Tip: Approving an excuse request automatically updates the member's status to Excused — unless they were already marked Present or Late (an approved excuse won't overwrite confirmed attendance).

3 Handling Excuse Requests

Members can submit excuse requests before events they cannot attend. Officers review these requests and approve or deny them.

Review Request

Read the member's reason for requesting an excuse

Approve

Mark the member as excused for the event

Deny

Reject the excuse (member will need to attend or be marked absent)

Note: Members are notified when their excuse request is approved or denied. Be consistent with excuse policies to ensure fairness.

4 Attendance Dashboard

The attendance dashboard (Officers → Attendance → Dashboard) gives a chapter-wide view of attendance trends. Use it to identify members who may need follow-up.

5 Best Practices

  • 1 Take attendance promptly - Record attendance during or immediately after the event for accuracy
  • 2 Review excuses regularly - Don't let excuse requests pile up; review them before the event when possible
  • 3 Be consistent - Apply the same standards to all excuse requests to ensure fairness
  • 4 Follow up on absences - Reach out to members with multiple unexcused absences
