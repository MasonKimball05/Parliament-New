1 Creating an Announcement

To create a new announcement, navigate to Officers → Manage Announcements and click "Create Announcement".

Announcement Fields

  • Title: A clear subject line for your announcement
  • Content: The body of your announcement (supports formatting)
  • Priority: Normal, Important, or Urgent
  • Target Audience: Who should receive this announcement

2 Email Delivery Options

Announcements can be delivered both as in-app notifications and via email. You have control over how emails are sent.

Send Email

Members receive an email in addition to the in-app notification. Best for important updates.

In-App Only

Members only see the notification in Parliament. Best for routine updates.

Tip: Be mindful of email frequency. Sending too many emails can lead to members ignoring important messages.

3 Scheduling Announcements

You can schedule announcements to be published at a future date and time. This is useful for preparing announcements in advance.

How Scheduled Emails Work

  • Immediate publish + Send Email: You'll see a confirmation popup showing exactly who will receive the email before sending.
  • Scheduled publish + Send Email: Emails are automatically sent when the scheduled time arrives. The system checks every 5 minutes for announcements ready to send.
  • In-app notifications: These are also sent automatically when the announcement becomes published.

Admin Feature: Admins can view pending scheduled emails and manually trigger them from Admin v2 → Email Logs if needed.

4 Targeting Your Audience

You can target announcements to specific groups of members:

All Members

Send to everyone in the chapter

Officers Only

Send only to chapter officers

Committee Members

Send to members of a specific committee
