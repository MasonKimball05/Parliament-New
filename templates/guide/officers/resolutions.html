Resolution Management

Track and document chapter decisions and their impacts

What are Resolutions?

Resolutions are formal records of legislation that has passed. They document what was decided and which sections of your governing documents were affected.

1 Understanding Resolutions

When legislation passes, it may affect various sections of your chapter's governing documents (constitution, bylaws, standing rules, etc.).

A resolution contains:

  • Title: A descriptive name for the resolution
  • Description: Detailed explanation of what was decided
  • Passed Date: When the resolution was approved
  • Section Impacts: Which document sections are affected
  • Related Legislation: Link to the original legislation

2 Creating a Resolution

After legislation passes, create a resolution to document the change for future reference.

Steps to create:

  1. 1 Navigate to Officers → Resolutions
  2. 2 Click "Create Resolution"
  3. 3 Enter the resolution title and description
  4. 4 Select the date it was passed
  5. 5 Link to the related legislation (if applicable)
  6. 6 Click "Save"

3 Documenting Section Impacts

Section impacts track which parts of your governing documents are affected by a resolution.

Impact Types

  • Added: New section created
  • Modified: Existing section changed
  • Removed: Section deleted
  • Replaced: Section completely rewritten

Document Types

  • Constitution
  • Bylaws
  • Standing Rules
  • Policies

Adding section impacts:

  1. Open the resolution
  2. Click "Add Section Impact"
  3. Select the document type
  4. Enter the section number/name
  5. Choose the impact type (added, modified, etc.)
  6. Describe the specific changes

4 Viewing Resolution History

The resolution list shows all documented decisions with filtering and search capabilities.

You can filter by:

  • Date range
  • Document type affected
  • Keyword search

Tip: Use resolutions to quickly answer questions like "When was the dues amount changed?" or "What happened to Article V, Section 3?"
