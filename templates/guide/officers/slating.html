Slating & Elections

Learn how to run officer elections and manage the slating process

Quick Actions

Slating Dashboard

Overview

The slating process allows chapters to collect officer applications, review candidates, build a slate of officers, and hold elections. The process is managed by the Slating Committee.

Election Phases

1 Nominations Open: Members apply for positions
2 Under Review: Committee reviews applications and conducts interviews
3 Slate Building: Committee assigns candidates to positions
4 Voting Open: Chapter votes on the slate
5 Results Published: Election results announced

1 Applying for Positions

When nominations are open, eligible members can apply for officer positions through the slating dashboard.

Application Requirements

  • Position Preferences: Select positions ranked by preference (1st, 2nd, 3rd choice)
  • GPA: Report your GPA with verification screenshot
  • Application Questions: Answer required questions about qualifications and goals

Tip: You can save your application as a draft and return to complete it later. Click "Save Draft" to preserve your progress.

2 Committee Review Process

The slating committee reviews all applications and conducts interviews with candidates.

Application Review

Committee members review submitted applications, verify GPA, and assess qualifications.

Interviews

Schedule and conduct interviews with candidates. Record notes and ratings for each interview.

Application Statuses

Draft
Submitted
Under Review
Approved

3 Building the Slate

After reviewing applications and conducting interviews, the committee assigns candidates to positions to create the final slate.

Slate Builder Features

  • Drag & Drop: Assign candidates to positions by dragging
  • Position Preferences: See each candidate's ranked preferences
  • GPA Verification: View verified GPA status for each candidate
  • Committee Approval: Slate must be approved before voting opens

4 Voting Process

Members vote on the slate as a whole. Votes are cast anonymously using a secret ballot system.

Approve

Accept the entire slate

Reject

Object to specific positions

Abstain

Choose not to vote

Password Required: You must enter your password to verify your identity when casting a vote. This ensures vote authenticity while maintaining ballot secrecy.

Approval Threshold

The slate must receive the required approval percentage to pass. If the slate fails, the chapter may have additional voting attempts. If all attempts fail, individual position voting may be used.

5 Results & Transition

Once voting concludes and the slate is approved, results are published and the officer transition process begins.

After Election: Newly elected officers will receive notifications about their positions. Current officers should work with incoming officers to ensure a smooth transition of responsibilities.

For Slating Committee Members

Committee members have additional management capabilities:

  • Review and rate applications
  • Schedule and manage interviews
  • Build and edit the slate
  • Manage voting periods and transitions
  • View live voting tally
