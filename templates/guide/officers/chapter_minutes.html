Chapter Minutes

Record and publish meeting minutes with motions and attendance

Quick Actions

Manage Minutes

1 Creating Minutes

To create new chapter minutes, go to Officers → Chapter Minutes and click "New Minutes".

Required Information

  • Title: A descriptive name (e.g., "Weekly Chapter Meeting")
  • Date: When the meeting took place
  • Start Time: When the meeting was called to order
  • Linked Event (optional): Connect to a chapter event to sync attendance

2 Using the Editor

The editor has three collapsible sections: Attendance, Meeting Minutes (the text area), and Publish. Each section can be expanded independently.

Insert Buttons (Meeting Minutes section)

Place your cursor in the text area where you want to insert, then use the toolbar buttons:

Motion / Vote
Record a formal motion with author, result, and counts
Section Header
Opens a named section box in the PDF
End Section
Closes the current section box

Save Draft vs. Save Attendance vs. Publish

  • Save Draft — saves the minutes text, sections, and motions. Does not affect who can see it.
  • Save Attendance — saves attendance separately. Must be done before publishing if you want attendance in the PDF.
  • Publish — generates the PDF and makes minutes visible to members. Attendance is saved automatically before the PDF is generated.

3 Using Section Headers

Section headers create visually distinct boxes in the PDF output. They're perfect for organizing officer reports, committee updates, and other structured content.

How Section Headers Work

  1. Click "Add Section Header" and enter a title (e.g., "President's Report")
  2. Add content below the header using text blocks or motions
  3. Click "End Section" to close the boxed area
  4. Everything between the header and end marker appears in a styled box in the PDF

Common Section Header Uses

President's Report
VP Reports
Committee Updates
Old Business
New Business
Announcements

Tip: You can nest content freely within a section. Add text, motions, or even multiple paragraphs before ending the section.

4 Markdown Formatting

The minutes text area supports Markdown formatting. Formatting is reflected in the final published PDF.

Inline Formatting

**bold** bold
*italic* italic
***bold italic*** bold italic
~~strikethrough~~ strikethrough
`inline code` code

Block Formatting

# Heading 1 Large heading
## Heading 2 Medium heading
- item Bullet list
1. item Numbered list
> text Blockquote
--- Horizontal rule

Tip: A collapsible formatting reference is built into the editor — look for the "Formatting Help" toggle above the text area.

5 Recording Motions & Votes

Use the motion block to record formal votes taken during the meeting. Each motion captures comprehensive details about the parliamentary procedure.

Motion Types

Main Motion
Amendment
Point of Order
Custom

Motion Details

Author & Second

Record who made the motion and who seconded it. You can select from chapter members or enter names manually.

Vote Method

Choose how the vote was conducted: voice vote, show of hands, roll call, or secret ballot.

Vote Counts

Record the number of votes for, against, and abstentions if a counted vote was taken.

Caucus

Track if a caucus was held, its duration, and whether it was moderated or unmoderated.

Vote Results

Passed
Failed
Tabled
Withdrawn

6 Tracking Attendance

Expand the Attendance section in the editor to mark who attended. When you click Save Attendance, it saves independently from the minutes text. If the minutes are linked to an event, changes sync to that event's attendance records.

Present
Attended meeting
Late
Arrived late
Absent
Did not attend
Excused
Approved absence

Linked Event: When minutes are linked to an event, attendance changes sync both ways. Excuse requests submitted for the event will appear in the minutes attendance section.

7 Publishing Minutes

When your minutes are complete, you can publish them to make them available to chapter members. Publishing generates a formatted PDF and adds it to Chapter Documents.

Download PDF

Preview or download the formatted PDF before publishing

Publish

Make minutes available in Chapter Documents with visibility controls

Editing Published Minutes: You can still edit minutes after publishing. Changes are tracked, and the PDF is automatically regenerated. An edit notice will appear in the document.
