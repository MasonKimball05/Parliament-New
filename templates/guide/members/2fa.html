Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security to your account

Why use Two-Factor Authentication?

2FA protects your account even if someone learns your password. You'll need both your password AND your phone to log in, making unauthorized access much harder.

1 What is Two-Factor Authentication?

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second step to your login process. After entering your password, you'll also need to enter a code from your phone.

How it works:

Password
Something you know
Phone Code
Something you have
Logged In
Secure access

2 Setting Up 2FA

You'll need an authenticator app on your phone. Popular options include:

Google Authenticator
iOS & Android
Authy
iOS & Android
Microsoft Authenticator
iOS & Android

Setup steps:

  1. 1 Download an authenticator app on your phone
  2. 2 Go to Preferences → Security
  3. 3 Click "Enable Two-Factor Authentication"
  4. 4 Scan the QR code with your authenticator app
  5. 5 Enter the 6-digit code from your app to verify
  6. 6 Save your backup codes in a safe place

3 Backup Codes

Backup codes let you access your account if you lose your phone. Each code can only be used once.

Important: Save your backup codes somewhere safe (password manager, printed in a secure location, etc.). Without these codes, losing your phone could lock you out of your account.

Good places to store backup codes:

  • Password manager (1Password, Bitwarden, etc.)
  • Printed and stored in a safe or lockbox
  • Encrypted notes app

4 Logging In with 2FA

Once enabled, you'll be asked for a code after entering your password.

  1. Enter your username and password as usual
  2. Open your authenticator app
  3. Enter the 6-digit code shown (it changes every 30 seconds)
  4. Click "Verify" to complete login

Tip: The code changes every 30 seconds. If your code is about to expire, wait for the next one to avoid timing issues.

5 Disabling 2FA

If you need to disable 2FA (not recommended), go to Preferences → Security and click "Disable Two-Factor Authentication."

Warning: Disabling 2FA makes your account less secure. Only do this if absolutely necessary, and re-enable it as soon as possible.
