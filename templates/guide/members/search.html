Global Search

Find anything in Parliament quickly

1 Using Global Search

The global search bar is located in the header. Click on it or use the keyboard shortcut to quickly search across Parliament.

How to access:

  • Click the search icon in the header

2 What You Can Search

Global search finds content across all areas of Parliament.

Members

Find members by name

Events

Search upcoming and past events

Legislation

Find legislation by title or content

Announcements

Search past announcements

Committees

Find committees by name

Documents

Search chapter documents

3 Search Tips

Be Specific

More specific terms give better results. "Budget meeting" is better than "meeting".

Use Keywords

Focus on important words. Skip common words like "the" or "a".

Recent Results First

Search results are sorted by relevance and recency.

Keyboard Shortcuts

/ Open search
Esc Close search
Enter Go to result
↑ ↓ Navigate results
