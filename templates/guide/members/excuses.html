Submitting Excuses

How to request an excused absence from events

1 When to Submit an Excuse

If you cannot attend a mandatory event, submit an excuse request before the event to avoid being marked absent.

Submit an excuse when:

  • You have a class conflict
  • You're sick or have a medical appointment
  • You have a work obligation
  • You have a family emergency
  • You're traveling

Not valid excuses:

  • Forgot about the event
  • Didn't feel like coming
  • Had other social plans
  • Submitting after the event (without prior reason)

2 How to Submit an Excuse

Steps to submit:

  1. 1 Find the event on the Calendar or Events page
  2. 2 Click on the event to view its details
  3. 3 Click "Submit Excuse" or "Request Excuse"
  4. 4 Select a reason category from the dropdown
  5. 5 Provide additional details about your absence
  6. 6 Submit your request

Tip: Submit your excuse as early as possible. Officers may be less likely to approve last-minute requests.

3 Checking Excuse Status

After submitting, an officer will review your excuse. You can check the status at any time.

Excuse Statuses

Pending
Awaiting review
Approved
You're excused
Denied
Not approved

4 Canceling an Excuse

If your plans change and you can now attend, you can cancel a pending excuse request.

To cancel:

  1. Go to the event details
  2. Find your pending excuse
  3. Click "Cancel Excuse"

Note: You can only cancel pending excuses. Once approved, contact an officer if you need to make changes.

5 Viewing Your Excuse History

You can view all your past excuses and attendance records from your profile or the attendance page.

Your excuse history shows:

  • Event name and date
  • Reason you provided
  • Approval status
  • Which officer reviewed it
