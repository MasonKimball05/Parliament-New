Calendar & Event Subscription

View events and sync with your calendar app

Quick Actions

View Calendar Calendar Subscription

1 Viewing the Calendar

The calendar shows all upcoming chapter events. Navigate to Calendar from the main menu to view it.

Calendar Features

  • Month View: See all events for the current month at a glance
  • Navigation: Use arrows to move between months
  • Event Details: Click any event to see full details
  • Color Coding: Events are colored by type for easy identification

2 Calendar Subscription (iCal)

Subscribe to the chapter calendar in your favorite calendar app (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, etc.) to automatically see all events.

How to subscribe:

  1. 1 Go to your Preferences page
  2. 2 Find the Calendar Subscription section
  3. 3 Copy your personal subscription URL
  4. 4 Add this URL to your calendar app as a subscription

Important: Your subscription URL is personal. Don't share it with others as it grants access to your calendar feed.

3 Adding to Popular Calendar Apps

G Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar settings
  2. Click "Add calendar" → "From URL"
  3. Paste your subscription URL
  4. Click "Add calendar"

Apple Calendar

  1. Open Calendar app
  2. File → New Calendar Subscription
  3. Paste your subscription URL
  4. Click "Subscribe"

O Outlook

  1. Open Outlook Calendar
  2. Click "Add calendar" → "Subscribe from web"
  3. Paste your subscription URL
  4. Click "Import"

4 Regenerating Your Calendar Token

If you accidentally share your calendar URL or want to revoke access, you can regenerate your token.

Warning: Regenerating your token will invalidate the old URL. You'll need to update your subscription in all calendar apps.

To regenerate:

  1. Go to Preferences
  2. Find the Calendar Subscription section
  3. Click "Regenerate Token"
  4. Update your calendar app with the new URL
