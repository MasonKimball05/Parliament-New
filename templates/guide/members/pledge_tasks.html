1 What is the Task Tracker?

The Pledge Task Tracker is your personal checklist of everything you need to complete during your pledge semester. Tasks are created and managed by the Education Committee (VPE and education chairs). Your progress is visible to the education committee at all times.

2 Reading Your Task List

Tasks are grouped by phase (General, Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3). Each group shows a progress bar and counts of required tasks completed.

Status indicators

Pending Task exists but hasn't been marked yet by a chair.
Awaiting Review You've submitted a quiz — a chair will review your answers.
Completed ✓ Marked complete by the education committee.
Incomplete Reviewed but not passed — check with your VPE for next steps.

Note: Non-required tasks count toward your points total but aren't blocking for initiation. Required tasks (marked with a star) must all be completed.

3 Taking a Quiz

Quiz tasks have a Take Quiz button on your task list. Quizzes are free-response — write out your answers in full sentences. There's no time limit.

1

Click "Take Quiz"

This appears on any quiz task that is live and hasn't been submitted yet.

2

Answer all questions

Every question must have an answer before you can submit. Take your time — you can scroll up and review questions before submitting.

3

Submit and wait for review

After submitting, the task shows "Awaiting Review." A chair will read your answers and mark the task completed or incomplete. You can view your submitted answers on the task page at any time.

Important: Quizzes can only be submitted once. Re-read the questions carefully before clicking Submit.

4 If Something Looks Wrong

A task isn't showing up

Tasks only appear once the education committee has activated them. If you think a task is missing, ask your VPE.

A task is marked Incomplete

Reach out to your VPE or the chair who reviewed it. They can add a reviewer note explaining what needs to be corrected, and re-mark it once you've addressed the feedback.

A quiz says "Not Available"

The quiz hasn't been activated yet, or it isn't assigned to you. Check with the education committee.
