Profile Management

Customize your profile and account settings

Quick Actions

Edit Profile Preferences

1 Profile Picture

Your profile picture helps other members recognize you. You can upload a custom photo or use your initials.

To change your profile picture:

  1. Go to Edit Profile
  2. Click on your current profile picture or the upload area
  3. Select an image file from your device (JPG, PNG, or GIF)
  4. Crop and adjust the image as needed
  5. Click Save to update

Tip: For best results, use a square image at least 200x200 pixels. Photos with your face clearly visible work best.

2 Username

Your username is used to log in to Parliament. You can change it at any time.

Good usernames

  • jsmith
  • john.smith
  • johnsmith2024

Avoid

  • Spaces or special characters
  • Very long usernames
  • Offensive terms

3 Preferred Name

Set your preferred name to control how your name appears throughout Parliament. This is separate from your legal name on file.

What the preferred name affects:

  • How your name appears in the member directory
  • Your display name on announcements and votes
  • Attendance records and reports
  • Committee and role assignments

4 Contact Information

Keep your contact information up to date so officers and members can reach you.

Email Address

Used for notifications, password resets, and official communications

Phone Number

Optional, visible to other members in the directory

5 Email Preferences

Control which emails you receive from Parliament. Access these settings from your Preferences page.

Email types you can control:

  • Announcement notifications
  • Event reminders
  • Legislation voting reminders
  • Committee updates
