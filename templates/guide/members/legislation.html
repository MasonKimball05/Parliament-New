Legislation & Voting

Understanding how to participate in chapter governance

Quick Actions

View Active Legislation Passed Legislation

1 What is Legislation?

Legislation is the formal process by which the chapter makes decisions. Members can propose changes to bylaws, create new policies, or make decisions that affect the entire chapter.

Bills

Proposals for new policies or actions that the chapter should take.

Amendments

Proposed changes to existing bylaws or the constitution.

2 How Voting Works

When legislation is up for a vote, all eligible members can cast their vote. Your vote is confidential and cannot be changed once submitted.

Voting Options

👍
Yea
Support the legislation
👎
Nay
Oppose the legislation
🤚
Abstain
Neither support nor oppose

Note: Some legislation may require a specific percentage of votes to pass (e.g., 2/3 majority for bylaw amendments). The required threshold is displayed on each piece of legislation.

3 Casting Your Vote

1

Find Active Legislation

Check your home page or the legislation section for items awaiting your vote.

2

Read the Full Text

Click on the legislation to read the complete text and any supporting documents.

3

Cast Your Vote

Select Yea, Nay, or Abstain and confirm your choice. Your vote cannot be changed.

Important: Make sure to vote before the deadline! You'll receive notifications about upcoming vote deadlines.

4 Submitting Your Own Legislation

Any member can submit legislation for the chapter to consider. Your proposal will be reviewed and put up for a vote.

Tips for Writing Effective Legislation

  • Be clear and specific about what you're proposing
  • Explain the reasoning behind your proposal
  • Consider how it will affect different members
  • Proofread carefully before submitting
