1 Accessing Notifications

The notification bell in the header shows your unread notification count. Click it to see your recent notifications.

Ways to access notifications:

  • Bell Icon: Click the bell in the top navigation bar
  • Notifications Page: View all notifications in one place
  • Email: Important notifications are also sent to your email

2 Notification Types

Parliament sends different types of notifications to keep you informed about chapter activity.

Announcements

Chapter-wide messages from officers

Event Reminders

Upcoming events you should attend

Legislation

New legislation ready for your vote

Kai Reports

Updates on reports you're involved in

3 Managing Notifications

Mark as Read

Click a notification to mark it as read, or use "Mark all as read"

Dismiss

Remove notifications you no longer need

Filter

Filter by type to find specific notifications

4 Email Notification Preferences

Control which notifications are sent to your email from your Preferences page.

Tip: Even if you disable email notifications, you'll still receive in-app notifications. Check Parliament regularly to stay up to date.
