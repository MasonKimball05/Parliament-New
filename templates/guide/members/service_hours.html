Service Hours

Track and submit your chapter service contributions

Quick Actions

My Service Hours

1 What are Service Hours?

Service hours track the time members contribute to community service and chapter service activities. Every member is expected to complete a minimum number of hours each semester (your service period).

📅 Service Events

Organized chapter service events on the calendar. Attend and get marked present — hours are applied automatically when the VPP finalizes attendance.

📝 Manual Submissions

Did service on your own? Submit a manual request with details and proof. An officer reviews and approves it.

2 Checking Your Progress

Your service hours dashboard shows your progress for the current period at a glance.

Hours logged vs. required

Your total approved hours for the current period alongside your individual requirement (which may differ from the chapter default if you have an override).

Submission history

A log of every submission — pending, approved, and rejected — with officer notes if provided.

The weekly chapter digest email (sent every Sunday) also includes your current hours progress so you don't have to log in to check.

3 Submitting Hours Manually

For service done outside of chapter-organized events, submit a manual request from your service hours dashboard.

1

Go to My Service Hours

Click "Submit Hours" on your dashboard.

2

Fill in the details

Include the activity name, date, number of hours, and a brief description of what you did. Be specific — vague submissions may be rejected.

3

Wait for approval

An officer will review and either approve or reject your submission. You'll be notified of the outcome. Approved hours are added to your total immediately.

Tip: Submit hours soon after completing service — don't wait until the end of the period. Batch submissions at the deadline are harder to verify.

4 Attending Service Events

Service events appear on the chapter calendar labeled with the hours they're worth. Showing up and being marked present is all you need to do — hours are applied automatically after the VPP finalizes the event.

What "finalized" means

After a service event, the VPP locks the attendance roster and clicks "Finalize." At that point, everyone marked present automatically receives a service hours submission credited to their account for the current period. You don't need to submit anything separately.
