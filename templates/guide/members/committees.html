Committees

Understanding chapter committees and how to participate

1 What are Committees?

Committees are smaller groups within the chapter that focus on specific areas of responsibility. They help distribute the work of running the chapter and give members opportunities to take on leadership roles.

Committee Structure

Chair

Leads the committee, runs meetings, and reports to the chapter

Members

Regular committee members who participate in committee work

Advisor

Officer or senior member who provides guidance (optional)

2 Your Committee Dashboard

When you're part of a committee, you have access to a committee dashboard with various features:

Committee Chat

Private messaging with committee members

Meeting Minutes

Records of committee meetings

Task Tracking

Committee to-dos and assignments

Committee Events

Events organized by the committee

3 Participating Effectively

Being an effective committee member helps the chapter run smoothly and provides you with valuable experience.

Attend Meetings

Show up to committee meetings regularly and come prepared

Take Initiative

Volunteer for tasks and bring new ideas to the committee

Communicate

Stay active in the committee chat and respond to messages promptly

Follow Through

Complete assigned tasks on time and update the committee on progress

4 Joining a Committee

Interested in joining a committee? Here's how to get involved:

How to Join

  • 1. Check which committees are looking for members
  • 2. Express your interest to the committee chair or your chapter officers
  • 3. Once added, you'll have access to the committee dashboard

Tip: Being on a committee is a great way to build leadership skills and demonstrate your commitment to the chapter. It's also excellent preparation if you're interested in running for an officer position in the future!

5 Committee Documents

Each committee has its own document library — meeting minutes, agendas, reports, and policies — found at Committee → Documents. Whether you can see or upload a given document depends on your committee permissions and the document's own visibility.

🔒 Committee Only
Default visibility
Only committee members can see it
✓ Published to Chapter
Published documents
Visible chapter-wide, not just to the committee

What you can do, by role

  • Anyone with document-upload permission can Publish to Chapter or Unpublish a document
  • Committee chairs can Replace File to upload a new version — the old one is kept in Version history with an optional note on what changed, not overwritten
  • Chairs and the committee's VP can Delete a document entirely

Note: Version history and Replace File only appear if your chapter has version tracking turned on. If you don't see them, that's expected — not every chapter uses it.
