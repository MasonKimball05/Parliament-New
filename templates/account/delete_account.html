What happens right away

Your account is closed. You're signed out of the website and the app on every device, and you can't sign in again. Your password, passkeys, authenticator and backup codes, app sign-ins, API tokens and calendar feed stop working.

What is permanently removed after {{ purge_days }} days

Your email addresses and phone number, social handles, profile photo, bio, majors, minors and concentrations, your sign-in history, page visits, settings and onboarding progress.

What the chapter keeps, and why

The chapter keeps a permanent membership record: your name, roll number, pledge class, house and family line, initiation and graduation details, roles you held, and your part in chapter business — votes, attendance, excuses, minutes, service hours, committee and slating records, quotes and chat messages. These are the chapter's records of who was a member and what it decided, so they stay. You'll be shown as a former member, without contact details or a photo.
